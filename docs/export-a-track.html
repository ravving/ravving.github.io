    Using ravving / guide/export-a-track.md

    Export a track and play it back

    A WAV, FLAC, OGG or MP3 with tags and a cover, then the music player, or a .ravv bundle to share.

    Turn your project into a song file: a WAV, FLAC, OGG or MP3 with its tags and a cover. Then play it in the music player, or send it somewhere.

    Open EXPORT

    Open PROJECT ▾ in the header and tap EXPORT.

    Name it and tag it

    • FILE NAME and TITLE.
    • ARTIST and COMPOSER start from your profile's author, set in MENU → SETTINGS.
    • ALBUM / MIXTAPE and GENRE offer the ones you typed last.
    • The cover is drawn for you from the title and the artist.

    Choose the format and where it goes

    • FORMAT: WAV 24-BIT, FLAC LOSSLESS, OGG or MP3.
    • USB: DEVICE ONLY, or a copy to a stick that's plugged in.
    • COMPUTER: send it to one of your computers too, once it's written.

    Choose how long

    • WHAT: LOOP plays the section on screen for BARS × LOOPS. Knobs 1 and 2 set them.
    • WHAT: SONG records the whole song, start to end.
    • TAIL adds a few seconds at the end for the echoes to ring out. Knob 3 sets it.

    The dialog shows how long the track will be.

    Export

    Tap EXPORT TRACK. ravving plays your project from its first beat and records it, so keep quiet: the speakers are the mix. A bar shows how far it's got, and CANCEL stops it.

    When it's done, the dialog closes. The track is in the library's exports, with its cover and details beside it.

    Your first release! Give it a proper title before you send it out.

    Play it back

    Open MENU → MUSIC PLAYER. Your new track is at the top of TRACKS.

    • ▶ plays a track, and + adds it to the queue.
    • SEND… sends it to a USB stick, a computer or your phone.
    • The big knob sets the volume, and knob 1 moves through the track.
    • BACK leaves the player.

    Take the whole project along

    EXPORT .RAVV saves a bundle: the project with every sample it uses. Another ravving opens it from PROJECT ▾ → IMPORT and plays it exactly the same.
