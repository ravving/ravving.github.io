    ravving / the documentation

    How everything works

    Learn ravving at your own pace. New here? Start with Your first beat. Looking for one key or page? The manual has them all, and the search box finds the rest.

    Play

    Get ravvingInstall ravving from the store in the Zynthbox app, start it, and go back to Zynthbox when you like. Your first beatFrom silence to a saved project in a few minutes: drums, a bass line, play and save. The machinesEvery instrument page and the engines and sounds it can play, all of them open source. Load a presetPick a preset from ravving or a USB stick, choose what comes along, and undo it. Turn your loop into a songCopy a section, change it, and play the parts in order: sections and the SONG page in a few steps. Play it liveSwitch parts on the beat, push the LIVE FX pads, play over the top, and record your set. Export a track and play it backA WAV, FLAC, OGG or MP3 with tags and a cover, then the music player, or a .ravv bundle to share. Film your sessionLIVE REC: a video of the screen or a camera with the sound in sync, or the sound alone, with your voice over it. Send a file to your phoneA QR code on the screen, the phone's camera, and the file is in its Downloads: no app, no pairing. When something's wrongNo sound, a dark screen, Bluetooth, USB sticks and recording: what to check, and what to do. The manualThe five pages and what plays on them, the shortcuts, undo, sound sources, presets, templates, sections and the song, exports, LIVE REC, files, USB sticks, Bluetooth, the RECORDER, the issues center.

    Make your own

    Make a skin setThe colours of the eight instrument pages as one JSON file: every key, the contrast rules, a checker to download, and how to add the set to ravving. Make an icon packYour own drawings for every icon ravving shows: the names and where each one shows, the drawing rules, a tool to build and check a pack, and how to share it.

    The pattern packs

    The genre packsThe pattern preset packs for a USB stick: what each preset is, its tempo, its sound, the scale it is written in and the template it ships with.
