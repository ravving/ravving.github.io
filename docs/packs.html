    The pattern packs / docs/packs.md

    The genre packs

    The genre packs you get with GET MORE: what each preset is, its tempo, its sound, the scale it is written in and the template it ships with.

    Twenty-five bands to download. Get one, and the drums, bass, lead and chords play your genre, in your key. The packs don't come installed with ravving: each one is free to download, use and remix. Each is written in a style, often after the bands and records that define it.

    Group Packs
    Genres Classical, Hip-Hop, Pop, Rock, Trap, Reggaeton, Punk, Tropical, Cumbia, Folk, Heavy Metal, Black Metal, Post-Punk
    Moods and eras Gregorian (old gothic), Vuelo (nu-disco after Flight Facilities), Hollywood (Lana Del Rey's beats under old-Hollywood strings)
    One song Body (after Grimes' Be a Body, its intro above all)
    Soul R&B (after Jorja Smith and Snoh Aalegra), Droon (after Sonder)
    Solos Solos (ballads after Coltrane's Ballads and Chet Baker), Bebop (fast horn lines after Parker, Coltrane and Baker), Fukui (piano trio after Ryo Fukui's Scenery)
    Jazz-pop Meller (after Petite Meller's Baby Love and Backpack: the horns, the drive)
    Others Jazz, and Piig, a smaller style pack

    Most packs have five presets each for the drums, the bass, the lead and the chords. Solos, Bebop, Body and Fukui have six, and Piig has three.

    Play a pack

    • Get it: MENU → GET MORE → PACKS, then GET on a pack. Its presets join each instrument's presets dialog under the pack's genre, and its templates join TEMPLATES.
    • Try one first: a presets dialog's SOURCE · ONLINE plays any pack's preset straight from the catalog, without downloading the rest.
    • On a USB stick: the catalog's page has each pack as a zip. Unzip its ravving folder to a stick's root, plug it in, and pick SOURCE · USB.

    The notes follow your key

    The notes are written as scale degrees, so a pack plays in whatever key and scale you set. Each pack sticks to one scale, so its four instruments play together:

    • Natural Minor: Hip-Hop, Trap, Reggaeton, Piig, Heavy Metal, Black Metal, Post-Punk, Vuelo, R&B, Droon, Hollywood, Fukui.
    • Major: Classical, Pop, Rock, Punk, Tropical, Cumbia, Folk, Solos, Bebop, Meller.
    • Dorian: Gregorian, Body.

    Tropical's minor styles (salsa, bachata) and Cumbia's (villera, chicha) sit on degree 6, the relative minor. That keeps each pack in one key: in C major, they play in A minor.

    Notes from outside the scale use ALTER, like rock's ♭3 and ♭7 or trap's ♭2. Chords from outside the key use chromatic voicings.

    Tempo and swing

    A pattern doesn't store its tempo or swing. The tables give each preset's tempo in quarter-note BPM on the 16-step grid. Every preset plays straight (SWING 50), except these:

    Preset SWING
    Hip-Hop · Golden Era Swing 58–62
    Hip-Hop · Boom Bap, Lo-Fi Lazy and their bass, lead and chords 54–60 if you like; straight works
    Hip-Hop · Jazz Rap, Jazz Rap Walk 60–66
    Rock · Half-Time Shuffle about 66, the triplet shuffle
    Piig · every preset 56–60, the lazy MPC swing
    Solos · every preset 54–58 on SWING 8, a ballad's lilt; straight works
    Bebop · every preset 58–64 on SWING 8; Trane Countdown and Trane Drive straight to 54
    R&B · every preset 54–58 on the sixteenths, the lazy pocket
    Droon · every preset 56–60 on the sixteenths; the Care set 60–64 on SWING 8
    Hollywood · every preset 52, a hair of lazy swing under the strings; straight works
    Fukui · the Scenery and Part B sets 56–62 on SWING 8; the Cascade, Slip, Waltz and Climax sets straight
    Meller · the Backpack set 64–68 on SWING 8, a hard shuffle

    SWING belongs to the project, so every machine swings together: set it with knob 2 in the header's TEMPO group, and tap its caption to swing the 8ths, SWING 8 (Tempo and swing).

    Feel: velocity, nudge and length

    Notes can carry their own velocity and nudge, like SELECT's knobs 2 and 3 set them. The packs use that for feel:

    • palm-muted chugs and tremolo strokes,
    • a fiddle's bow and a chant's swell,
    • grace notes snapped in early,
    • a dub bass sitting behind the beat.

    3/4 and 6/8

    Some presets are in 3/4 or 6/8, like Classical's waltz trio (Waltz, Waltz Oom and Waltz Pah-Pah). Three 16-step bars hold four 12-step bars, so they loop together. The tempo counts a quarter as four steps, so a jig at a dotted quarter of 110 reads 165.

    Make your own

    Each pack is a script in the catalog, ravving_repo, and its guide for contributors has the format, the tools and the check a pack goes through. Made one? Send it as a pull request.

    Sounds

    Every preset says which sound it was written for, and loads it when WITH ITS SOUND is on. All of them are already there: nothing needs installing.

    • Drums: the drum machine kits, plus acoustic kits picked from a General MIDI soundfont. Some, like the orchestra, cumbia and Latin kits, map their rows to other instruments: the orchestra kit's TOM and HAT are timpani, and the Latin kits swap in congas, bongos, güiro and cowbell.
    • Bass, lead and chords: soundfont instruments, and "808 · Monosynth", a sub bass on Calf Monosynth that glides between notes.

    Drum kits don't follow the key, so the orchestra kit's timpani sound best in C.

    Soundfont sounds don't glide: a SLIDE only overlaps the notes. That's why the 808 lines use "808 · Monosynth".
