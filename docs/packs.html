The pattern packs / data/secret/README.md

The genre packs

Seventeen packs of pattern presets for a USB stick: what each preset is, its tempo, its sound, the scale it is written in and the template it ships with.

Sixteen genre packs plus the jazz pack: classical, hip-hop, pop, rock, trap, reggaeton, punk, tropical, cumbia, folk, heavy metal, black metal, post-punk, Gregorian (old gothic), vuelo (Flight Facilities' nu-disco) and solos (six horn solos after Parker, Coltrane and Baker). Each has five pattern presets for each of the drums, the bass, the lead and the chords (solos six). A smaller style pack, piig, has three of each. They are not installed with ravving. To play one, copy a pack's ravving folder to the root of a USB stick: the presets dialog's SOURCE · USB plays them straight from there (PresetExchange::stickPatterns / loadStickPattern), and the USB page can import them.

data/secret/<genre>-presets/build.py                     the generator (python3 build.py)
data/secret/<genre>-presets/ravving/presets/<drums|bass|lead|chords>/patterns/<Genre> · <Name>.json
data/secret/patternkit.py                                shared by the genre build.py files (not the jazz one)
data/secret/validate.py                                  checks every pack against ravving's parsers

Each file is {"ravving": "preset", "type": "pattern", "library": "<x>-presets.json", "preset": {"name", "genre", "chart", "sound"}}. The chart is the shipped libraries' format. sound is the sound the pattern was written for: {"soundSource": …} for the bass, lead and chords, {"kit": …} for the drums (a subset of Machine::soundState()).

The notes are scale degrees. They follow the session's root and scale. Each genre is written for one scale, so its four instruments play together:

  • Natural Minor: hip-hop, trap, reggaeton, piig, heavy metal, black metal, post-punk, vuelo.
  • Major: classical, pop, rock, punk, tropical, cumbia, folk, solos.
  • Dorian: Gregorian.
  • Tropical's minor styles (salsa, bachata) and cumbia's (villera, chicha) sit on degree 6, the relative minor, so each pack stays in one key. In C major they play in A minor.

Notes from outside the scale use ALTER (for example rock's ♭3/♭7, trap's ♭2 and raised 7th). Chords from outside the key use chromatic c voicings.

Tempo and swing are not stored in a pattern. The tables give each preset's tempo, in quarter-note BPM on the 16-step grid. Every preset plays straight (SWING 50) except these:

Hip-Hop · Golden Era Swing 58–62

Each pack ships a template, or more (pack.template(…), patternkit.write_template(); the projects dialog's TEMPLATES, ravving/presets/templates/<genre>.json on the stick, a second one under its own name=): the tempo, swing, key and LIVE FX its presets are best played with. Choosing one starts a new project with them. The tempo is the middle of the pack's preset tempos; the key is the one a pack was made for where it says so, else a usual key of the style.

The notes may carry their own velocity and nudge (SELECT's knobs 2 and 3, the chart's VELOCITY and NUDGE lanes by step): patternkit writes them from a note token's @64 and <10 / >10 (10 % of a step early / late). The packs made since use them: palm-muted chugs, tremolo down- and up-strokes, a fiddle's bow, grace notes snapped in early, a dub bass behind the beat, a chant's swell. A note's LENGTH is its _ steps (HOLD), as before.

6/8 and 3/4 in the new packs work as the waltz trio does (below): three 16-step bars hold four 12-step bars, and the tempo counts a quarter of four steps (a jig at a dotted quarter of 110 is "165").

The waltz trio is in 3/4. Classical · Waltz, Waltz Oom and Waltz Pah-Pah are three 16-step bars long, which hold four 12-step waltz bars, so the three loop together.

Every sound named is a source in data/kits.json (or a kit path), and validate.py checks this. I added two kinds of entries to kits.json, all from the FluidR3Mono_GM.sf3 already on the device (/zynthian/zynthian-data/soundfonts/SF2 Instruments/):

  • Bass sources: Picked bass 0:34, Slap bass 0:36, Sine sub 8:80, Contrabass 0:43, Cello 0:42.
  • Lead sources: Violin 0:40, Harpsichord 0:6, Trumpet 0:56, Guitar · Overdrive 0:29, Marimba 0:12, Steel drums 0:114, Pan flute 0:75, Accordion 0:21, Music box 0:10, Guitar · Clean 0:27 (champeta's soukous lead).
  • Bass source "808 · Monosynth": a jalv source on Calf Monosynth, the lead machine's own engine (a second instance on the bass track). Its controls are real port values, checked against the plugin's lv2info dump in engine-mods/calf_monosynth_mod/docs/engine-symbols.txt:
  • Sound: osc 1 Sine (o1_wave 3), plus 15 % of a triangle an octave up (o2_wave 4, o2_xpose 12, o12_mix 0.15) for the harmonics an 808's saturation gives.
  • Filter: 12 dB low-pass at 400 Hz that follows the key, opened by a 60 ms filter envelope (env2cutoff 2400 cents): the click of the attack.
  • Amp envelope: 1 ms attack, 1.8 s decay to 0.3, 250 ms release.
  • Glide: legato 3 (Fingered Legato) with portamento 80 ms, so only overlapping notes (a SLIDE) glide.
  • Off: LFO-to-pitch, LFO-to-filter, the mod wheel; master 0.5.
  • What it cannot do:
    • No pitch drop. The Monosynth's modulation matrix is LV2 state, not ports (the mod's RESEARCH.md), so kits.json can't set a pitch envelope.
    • No drive port. Turn on the bass machine's DRIVE for the saturation.
    • The bass page's knobs (Nekobi's) don't apply to it.

Nothing needs installing on the device. The device's font is MuseScore 2.3.2's FluidR3Mono_GM.sf3. It matches by sha256 cfcd66d89e8386823400eca64934b14fbea7bf48ba1f00d21189af1262794ec2, the hash in zynthbox-qml's config/soundfonts.json, entry SF_00022. A byte-identical copy is in ~/Downloads/ravving-soundfonts/fluidr3mono/, from https://github.com/musescore/MuseScore/raw/v2.3.2/share/sound/FluidR3Mono_GM.sf3. Its licence is MIT (Frank Wen 2000–2002, mono version Michael Cowgill 2014–16; see LICENSE-FluidR3Mono.md there).

How the new sounds were checked. Every new preset was rendered offline from that copy with tools/soundfont.py render. All of them sound, with peak levels from 0.02 to 0.20 and none silent. The kit rows were also worked out with a port of DrumKit::fromSoundfont. validate.py checks that every FluidR3Mono bank:program in kits.json exists, whenever the copy is present.

Limits of these sounds: FluidSynth sources don't glide; a SLIDE only overlaps the notes. The 808 lines therefore use "808 · Monosynth", which does glide. "Sine sub" (8:80) stays registered, but no preset uses it any more.
