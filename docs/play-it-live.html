    Play it live

    Switch parts on the beat, push the LIVE FX pads, play over the top, and record your set.

    Your song has its parts. Now perform it: switch parts on the beat, throw the mix around with the LIVE FX pads, play over the top, and record the whole set.

    Switch parts on the beat

    While the music plays, pick another section and it takes over on the next bar line, exactly on time. Nothing drops or doubles.

    • X and Y, beside the big knob, go to the previous and next section, from any page.
    • ALT + step keys 1–16 pick sections 1–16, while the section keys are in the header.
    • Tap a section key in the header, or on the SONG page.

    The section's key blinks while it waits, and the header's plate sweeps in its colour when it lands. Press X or Y again before the switch lands to move the pick further along.

    Wait for the end of the part

    To finish the bars of the part that's playing first, open the SONG page and set SWITCH to SECTION. A pick then waits for the part playing to finish its pass. BAR, the other choice, switches on the next bar.

    You can also line up to three parts ahead. Each plays once, then the next takes over, and the last one keeps looping.

    Bring in the LIVE FX pads

    The header starts with the section keys. Press ALT + MODE, or long-press the project plate, and the LIVE FX pads take their place. Do it again to bring the sections back.

    1. Hold ALT. The step keys light up orange, and the pads show their key numbers.
    2. Press step keys 1–8 for the eight pads in the header.
    3. Press step keys 9–16 for the eight more that show while you hold ALT.

    You can tap the pads on the screen too. ALT + ▼ goes to the next bank: MAIN, then MORE, thirty pads in all.

    Pads to start with

    • DROP, held on its key: the kick cuts out and the mix sweeps up. Let go for the drop.
    • STUTTER, held: the current 16th repeats. Drag on it for a shorter repeat.
    • LOOP 4, LOOP 8, held: the current beat or 8th repeats.
    • NO KICK: a tap takes every kick out, another brings them back.
    • LO PASS, HI PASS: a filter sweep, down or up, until you tap again.
    • ECHO, held: the mix goes into an echo, and rings out when you let go.

    On the screen, some pads act only while held. Others switch on with a tap, or stay on once held for half a second, DROP among them. The LIVE FX table has every pad.

    Play over the top

    Press MODE on a machine's page, and LIVE turns the step keys into something to play:

    • Drums: keys 1–8 play the rows, and key 9 held makes a hit an accent.
    • Bass and lead: the scale over all 16 keys. ▲ ▼ shift the octave.
    • Chords: the seven chords of your key.

    MODE goes on to STEP + LIVE, then OFF, and the step keys write the grid again. The MODE light shows which one is on.

    Solo a machine

    Long-press a machine's S to hear it alone. Every other solo drops. Tap S again to bring the band back.

    Record your set

    Press ALT + ● on any page, and everything the speakers play records as a WAV. The RECORD light turns red, and the header shows ● REC with the time. Press ALT + ● again to stop.

    Your set is in MENU → MUSIC PLAYER, ready to play back or send on. To film it as well, see Film your session.
