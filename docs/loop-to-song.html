    Turn your loop into a song

    Copy a section, change it, and play the parts in order: sections and the SONG page in a few steps.

    A loop gets you started. A song needs parts: an intro, a verse, a drop. In ravving, each part is a section, and the song is the order they play in.

    Open the SONG page

    Press ALT + 1 from any page. Press it again to go back to where you were.

    Your loop is section 1, named A. A new project's song already has it as its first part.

    Make a second part

    1. Tap ＋. It copies the section on screen: A becomes A′.
    2. The copy is now the one on screen, and it joins the end of the song.
    3. Press ALT + 1 to go back to your instrument page, and change something. Add a snare, a bass line, or take the kick out.

    Your edits go into the section on screen only, so A stays as it was. Tap NEW instead of ＋ for an empty section.

    Play the song

    1. Press ALT + 1 to open the SONG page again.
    2. Tap SONG at the top right, so PLAY plays the song from start to end. LOOP plays the section on screen only.
    3. Press PLAY.

    The page shows the part and bar playing, and the plate shows the part, like "2/2". The song stops at its end.

    Shape it

    • Repeats: pick a block in the song, and set ×1 to ×16 with + and −, or knob 2.
    • Order: pick a block, and move it with ALT + ◀ ▶.
    • Length: a section lasts as long as its longest pattern. Set LENGTH for a fixed 1–8 bars.
    • Names and colours: double-tap a section to rename it. COLOUR gives it another colour.

    ▶ PLAY FROM HERE starts the song at the block you picked.

    Give a part its own sound

    Every section plays the project's sounds, unless you give one a sound of its own: Rhodes chords in the verse, strings in the chorus.

    1. With the section on screen, tap the machine's nameplate to open its sound picker.
    2. Turn THIS SECTION on, at the end of the picker's top row.
    3. Choose a sound. The other sections keep theirs.

    The nameplate shows the sound after a §, and the section's key wears a ♪. The switch lands on the bar line, like the rest of the section. PROJECT'S SOUND in the picker takes it back.

    Verse, chorus, verse, drop. Now it's a song!

    Switch by hand while you play

    In LOOP, pick a section while the music plays. It takes over on the next bar line, exactly on time:

    • tap its key on the SONG page;
    • or press X or Y, beside the big knob, for the previous or next one, from any page;
    • or put the section keys in the header with ALT + MODE, then ALT + step keys 1–16.

    Record it

    PROJECT ▾ → EXPORT → WHAT: SONG records the whole song in one take.
