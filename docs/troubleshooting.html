    When something's wrong

    No sound, a dark screen, Bluetooth, USB sticks and recording: what to check, and what to do.

    Start with the ISSUES card in the menu. It reads ALL GOOD, or the number of issues lit in the colour of the worst one. Tap it for the list, worst first, each with what to do and a button where one helps, like RESTART RAVVING.

    Here are the common ones, by what you notice.

    No sound

    • Is a machine muted, soloed out or paused? Look at its S, M and ❚❚ on the strip, or open the MIXER with ✱.
    • Is the master down? ALT + the big knob sets it, from anywhere.
    • Where's the sound going? MENU → AUDIO OUTPUT picks the jacks, HDMI or a Bluetooth device. The sound comes out of one place only.
    • Still nothing? The issues center lists an engine that couldn't start, or audio connections that never appeared.

    The screen is black or very dim

    Open MENU and turn knob 1: it's the BRIGHTNESS slider.

    A tap doesn't change the pattern

    While the transport plays, LOCK keeps the note grids from writing, and flashes to tell you why. Tap LOCK in the pattern toolbar to edit anyway, or press STOP.

    Bluetooth

    A speaker or headphones won't connect

    A device that's asleep, waking up or busy with a phone gets called again three more times, over about 35 seconds. Its row reads "waking…" meanwhile.

    If ravving says NOT ANSWERING, free it from your phone, or turn it off and on, then connect again.

    Only one plays at a time

    Connecting a second speaker while another plays hands the sound over to it, and the first one disconnects.

    The sound is crunchy

    Open the BLUETOOTH dialog and switch SBC-XQ off for that speaker. Each device has its own chip, so your other headphones keep the finer sound.

    It went silent

    ravving routes the sound to it again by itself. If that doesn't bring it back, the issues center says the output plays no sound.

    USB sticks

    • Several sticks or partitions? The USB card names each one, and asks which to open.
    • It says read-only? That's a locked stick, or an installer image. Nothing is copied to it. Its page offers FORMAT, which erases the whole stick into one writable RAVVING drive. FORMAT and EJECT both ask for a second press.
    • It wouldn't mount? The issues center says so.

    Recording

    • MIC TOO HOT: the mic's peaks pass −6 dBFS. Turn the RECORDER's GAIN down.
    • The music doesn't duck under your voice: the voice over is for a voice. With the RECORDER on GUITAR or LINE, the input is simply mixed in.

    Still stuck?

    Tap RESTART RAVVING in the issues center. If ravving itself won't start, turn the MIKO off and on.
