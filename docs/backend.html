    Backend classes

    Every class of the backend library and how the others use it.

    Every class of the backend library (src/backend/), what it does and how the others use it. Each class has its full reference in the API reference.

    The drums play through libzynthbox's sketchpad tracks, patterns and synth slots, with ravving's C++ objects in the places where libzynthbox calls back into its host:

    Class Role Stands in for / ports
    Studio backend root (studio in QML): builds the machines from data/kits.json, transport (play/stop, tempo, swing, step), saves the sequence after edits; a preview clock on a desktop; the sampler's browser (sampleFiles(): kits.json sampleFolders, folders labelled short) and hold-to-hear previews (previewSample() / stopPreview(): a one-shot clip on SamplerSynth's global lane) sketchpad song + transport glue
    Machine the base of every machine: track (level, pan, effects, mute, ❚❚, kick cuts), pattern (bars, view bar following the playing bar while playing: follow, off when a bar is picked mid-play, back on when stopped; playhead, STEP cursor), presets, LIVE play/record, UNDO / REDO of the pattern (canUndo, undo(), canRedo, redo(): every edited whose content differs is a step back, 50 deep, changes within 0.5 s of the last joining its step (a knob turned, a handle dragged); the mix, sound and effects are left out, and so are a subclass's viewKeys() such as the sampler's bank; beginEditGroup()/endEditGroup() make a take one step; a project open starts it afresh); subclasses give steps, sound and velocities; SECTIONS: sectionContent() (the pattern part less soundState()'s keys and the viewKeys(), plus ❚❚ and ONCE) and applySectionContent() (laid over the machine's own by withSection(), the history started over; an empty map empties the pattern), sectionBars() / sectionSounds(); the samplers keep their banks' hits, not their samples (SamplerBanks) Its own length (bars, m_bars) is kept apart from what its clip plays: applyPlayLength() is the one place that sets the clip's patternLength and bankOffset, from playedBars(): one bar under ⟲ (the bar on screen), the section's span under ONCE (once, setSpan() from the studio; the bars past its own emptied by clearRest()), else its own; while it rests the playhead hides and a live hit is not written. new
    MonoMachine a mono line (one note per step) with ACCENT and SLIDE (the note lasts 1.5 steps and a legato engine glides into the next): page 3's AC 303 (bass, DISTRHO Nekobi: accent velocity 127 vs 80, Nekobi accents above 90) and page 4's THERE LEAD (lead, Calf Monosynth, legato + portamento); base note and velocities from kits.json; presets in scale degrees (bass-presets.json, lead-presets.json), RANDOM, octave; follows the header's key: a ROOT or SCALE change snaps every note to the nearest note of the new key (upwards on a tie), and the line's key is saved with it new
    ChordMachine page 2's POLY-6 on TAL NoiseMaker (6 voices, Juno chorus); a custom voicing is scale degrees, or chromatic (semitones from the chord base, chart letter c) when a note is outside the key, so a chart can carry a C7 in E♭ major; setStepNotes(step, "48,52,55,58") for scripts; live keys below 1000 are chord degrees, from 1000 on single scale degrees (noteKey, noteKeyForNote, noteOfKey: the NOTES tab's LIVE, written one note into the step's voicing by a take or STEP entry): each step holds a voicing, a diatonic chord (a degree stacked in thirds from the scale, triad or seventh per degree by the chord set: Classic, Triads, Sevenths) or a custom set of scale degrees (built in NOTES); a run of one voicing is one held chord; everything is in scale degrees, so a key, set or octave change revoices it all. Three views: CHORDS (setChord), NOTES (noteSets, toggleNote), SLOTS (four chordSlots of 1/2/4/8 bars, armedSlot, assignSlot, cycleSlotBars, clearSlot: editing one rebuilds the steps in order). LIVE keys and the chord keys play whole chords, a keyboard note plays its degree's chord; presets chord-presets.json ("1111666633337777", "x" + VOICINGS for custom) new
    SoundPresets an LV2 plugin's factory presets read from its bundle's presets.ttl (name, category from TAL's prefix, port values); a machine's kits.json soundPresets {file, keep} gives it soundPresets / soundPreset / loadSoundPreset(): the ports are set (but keep), the SOUND MODIFIERS take the values (adopt()), the name is saved, and an engine restart sends the sound again before the knobs new
    SoundControls a machine's SOUND MODIFIERS driving its engine's own ports, built from the machine's sound in kits.json (owned by Machine): cards of knobs, 0..1 mapped onto min..max linearly or with "curve": "log"; a knob with choices is a switch (snaps to i / (n − 1), ParamCard draws it as a WaveCycler) whose choices can each set several ports (TAL's JUNO wave: waveform + pulse width + levels). Bass: WAVE · FILTER (CUTOFF RESO ENV MOD) · VOICE (DECAY ACCENT TUNE); chords: FILTER (CUTOFF RESO LFO) · SHAPE (ATTACK LENGTH GLIDE) · TONE (WAVE SAW/JUNO/SQR/TRI, SPREAD, CHORUS OFF/I/II); lead: WAVE (SAW SQR SIN TRI) · FILTER (CUTOFF RESO ENV) · VOICE (GLIDE LENGTH). Machine::setExpression() plays kits.json expressions live (the ribbon's vibrato = Calf lfo2pitch) new
    JalvPlugin, JalvEngine one LV2 plugin in jalv (effects, and instruments with MIDI in); JalvEngine makes an instrument a machine's SoundEngine (a mono output feeds both sides) zynthian_engine_jalv.py
    DrumMachine one drum machine: kit rows, steps as subnotes of its track's PatternModel, accents, presets, per-row level/mute/solo (as velocities), level (track gain), pan (track passthrough), mute, audition, kit switching and reset new
    TakeRecorder a machine's REC (studio.take): a fresh take of what is played LIVE, counted in by the transport's first bar (stopped) or punched in at the loop's restart (playing), overdubbing loop after loop; holds the metronome on for the take new
    MidiInput external controllers (studio.midiIn; devices: the connected ones' names): notes from MidiRouter's hardware port (raw controller notes parked on unused track 9; every device's channels reset to the current track; bridge duplicates dropped), plug-in/out notices zynthbox-qml's MIDI controller handling (subset)
    VideoRecorder LIVE REC (video, "Live recordings" below): a video of the screen (x11grab) or a USB camera (v4l2) with the master's sound, or (mode "audio") the sound alone as a 24-bit WAV among the exports (finished with tags through Exporter::finishRecording), encoded by ffmpeg (libx264, two threads, nice 10; three quality tiers) into videos/<project> - <date>.mp4; the sound comes from AudioTap, the sync from the wall clock on both inputs (-copyts, -itsoffset = the tap's first-sample moment), the file goes to Matroska first and is remuxed once to MP4 with its head cut to the first frame; states off / ready / countdown / starting / recording / saving, takesKey gives it RECORD and STOP while VIDEO is on (Ravving::handleCuia); VIDEO SAVED sends it on (ComputerLink, a USB stick) new
    AudioTap ravving's JACK client ravving-video (stereo in): the master's last outputs copied on the audio thread into a 2^20-frame ring (21 s), drained by a plain thread into a named pipe ffmpeg reads as raw float; the first sample's CLOCK_REALTIME from jack_get_cycle_times; frames the ring could not hold are counted, never lost quietly; the mic inputs mixed in, plain or as a voice over (setVoiceOver(duckDb, liftDb): a 120 Hz high-pass and a +4 dB shelf from 3 kHz on the mic, its envelope followed, the master ducked while it speaks, the sum through a peak limiter at −1 dBFS, never clipped) new
    FileBrowser the FILES browser (files, FilesPopup): the library by kind ({key, label, about, usbKind, count}; projects through Projects, presets and templates through PresetExchange, the rest by folder and suffix from filesuffixes.h, shared with UsbLibrary), entries(kind) newest first, presetFile(id) to send a preset as one JSON file, remove(entry) through the owner (Projects, the preset library, the machine, Templates) or the file new
    Computers the user's computers over SSH (computers, SETTINGS · COMPUTERS): any number of ComputerLinks, each in its own settings group computers/<id> (a single-computer set-up of an older version moved into c1), add(host, user, password) (the same user@host set up again, not added twice), send(id, files), byId, current (the last added or set up), busy / phase, the links' sent / notify / setupFinished passed on with the id new
    ComputerLink one computer over SSH (a Computers entry): the device key (~/.config/ravving/ssh/id_ed25519) installed on the computer with ssh-copy-id and the password through SSH_ASKPASS (used once, never stored), ravving's own known_hosts, the Downloads folder asked with xdg-user-dir, send(files) by scp one at a time; statuses unset / setting-up / ready / unreachable / refused / failed new
    FileShare a file offered to a phone (share, SEND… → PHONE · QR CODE): a one-file HTTP server on QTcpServer (GET / HEAD at /d/<128 random bits>/<name>, Content-Disposition attachment, Range → 206, 64 kB chunks as the socket drains, 404 for anything else, port 8098 or the next free one) bound on every interface, the URL on the Wi-Fi address; offer(path) → the URL, stop(), 15 minutes at most; statuses idle / waiting / sending / done, sent, downloads, downloaded(name); the QR code is drawn by QrCodeItem (src/, Nayuki's qrcodegen vendored in src/backend/thirdparty) in QrPopup.qml. The same server carries the ravving page: setHelpFolder(dir) (the installed site/) keeps it listening for good and serves GET /help/<file> from that folder (index.html for /help/, no path above it, text types with a charset, pages no-cache and the rest cached 10 minutes), helpUrl = http://<address>:<port>/help/, what HelpPopup.qml (SETTINGS → HELP) draws as a QR code; and the library for a phone (openBrowse() → browseUrl, /b/<32 hex>/, 15 minutes after the phone's last request, closeBrowse()): the FileBrowser's kinds and entries as small self-styled pages (respondBrowse), a file served as an attachment from the path the browser lists, looked up by kind and name, never from the URL new
    MixRecorder the global recording (studio.mix): the master (GlobalPlayback) to recordings/ravving-<date>.wav through AudioLevels (refused while a sampler take records: AudioLevels runs one recording at a time); the music player lists these under "Recordings" new
    PadSamplerMachine page 5's PAD SAMPLER (studio.machines.pads, kind "pads"): 4 banks × 16 pads, each loaded pad one ClipAudioSource set up from the pad's params[tab][knob] (TRIM start / end / loop point, MIX pan, AMP ENV attack and decay / release, TUNE semi + fine, PLAY mono / mute group as exclusivity groups) and flags (NOTE ON = NonLooping, LOOP = Looping from the loop point, REVERSE = a reversed copy, MUTE; 16 LEVELS kept); pads 1–10 on track 7, 11–16 on a second track (kits.json secondTrack 8) whose pattern the machine mirrors (hits, length, loop, swing, mute) and whose live notes go out on it, both tracks' sample outputs wired into track 7's lane (JackWiring::setSampleOutputs(track, samples, laneTrack)); level as velocity; FILTER = the clip's equaliser bands (one, two for the 4-pole types, cutoff and Q from the knobs); the kit's knobs (PadKitControls, the SOUND MODIFIERS: KIT volume / semi / fine, 16 LEVELS type and switch); 16 LEVELS: the selected pad becomes the source, every pad plays it by velocity ramp or on sixteen level slices (notes 64–79, transposed by root note per TUNE semitones or the header's SCALE from pad 4), the hits meanwhile are the bank's level hits; audition = one pass; latch = the note held with the slice looping, unlatched after a short delay so the note-off is not ignored; PAD FX held on the pad played last (heldFx, fxPad): ECHO = the track's echo throw, TAPE STOP / SCRATCH = sweeps of the clip's speedRatio (a QVariantAnimation), STUTTER = a sixteenth-of-the-tempo retrigger, SKIP / REVERSE = the pad restarted with its start moved on / its reversed copy from the point reached, restored on release; erase pad, halve / double, UNMUTE ALL; presets pads-presets.json ("1"–"16"); playing and playPositions (per pad: whether its clip sounds and where it is in the file, from the clip's isPlaying / position signals) feed the grid's beam and the screen's playhead the MPC Sample
    SamplerMachine page 5's SAMPLE KING (studio.machines.sampler, track 6, "sample-trig"): 4 banks × 8 slots, each slot one ClipAudioSource (made through the bridge's exported constructor) on its track's sample slot i % 5, row i / 5, set up on its root slice: key zone = root note = baseNote + i, one-shot (a retrigger chokes it), PITCH = slice pitch, trim = start/length samples, 8 ms release; REVERSE plays a reversed copy rendered once in a worker (AudioFile::writeReversed, cached by path hash). Steps, accents and row mix as the drums (velocities); presets sampler-presets.json ("1"–"8": x/X). Published to the track as samples (SketchpadTrack::setSamples), its loaded slots' SamplerSynth ports wired into the lane (JackWiring::setSampleOutputs); playing / playPositions per slot as the PAD SAMPLER has new
    InputEffects the live effects on the device input (studio.input.effects): presets from data/input-fx.json (TAME, FUZZ VOICE, ROBOT, TELEPHONE, OCTAVE, AMP; OFF = the plain input), each a chain of jalv plugins (ravving-in-<preset>-<n>, started the first time it is picked and kept running) with fixed controls and three knobs mapped onto ports (linear or log); "bpm" delays follow the studio tempo. InputRecorder wires input → chain → monitor track and → the port recorder (JackWiring::setInputChain, which also makes the recorder connections, retried until a new plugin's ports exist): what is heard is what is recorded new
    AudioFile libsndfile helpers run in workers: info, writeReversed, transients (onsets on the 10 ms peak envelope for the wave editor's THRESHOLD chop), writeWindow (a trim to a new file, normalised to −1 dBFS when asked), processTake (a 12 dB/octave high-pass by mode: MIC 80 Hz, GUITAR 40, LINE 20; a noise gate set 12 dB over the take's own noise floor, up to −30 dB; 3:1 compression 15 dB under the peak; normalise to −1 dBFS; refuse takes under −50 dBFS; the last burst as the trim). On a real mic take it left the pauses at −48 dBFS where the first version (6:1, no gate) brought them up to −39 new
    InputPorts the input card's JACK capture ports found by the card, not a name: pw-dump gives the port whose alias is ":capture_FL/FR" and its serial; its JACK name is ":capture_FL-" when that exists, else the plain one (milo's two "Built-in Audio Stereo" cards swap the suffix between boots) new
    InputRecorder the device input (studio.input): MIC / GUITAR (mono: the jack with the signal, activeJack, followed between takes) / LINE set the ADC's PGA gain and mic bias through amixer (-- before the arguments: negative gains); armed connects the input to AudioLevels' port recorder, whose meter is level; MONITOR runs the input through its own track (kits.json input.track, 9) whose gain is monitorLevel; a take is the port recorder into a raw WAV, processed in a worker into samples/my-samples/ravving/ (takeReady(file, start, length)). Mode, gains and monitor level are the device's (QSettings ravving/input) new
    PresetLibrary the drum presets every machine shares: factory beats (data/drum-presets.json) and the user's own (saved next to the project) new
    Sections SECTIONS and the song (studio.sections): the sections (id, name, colour, length: its own 1–8 bars or the longest sounding pattern, which machines sound), the section on screen (current, edited live: pick() keeps the one left as edited and hands the machines the one picked), the song (entries of a section × repeats, songBars, each entry's startBar) and LOOP / SONG; add() (empty, after the current one), duplicate() (A → A′, same colour), rename, remove (the neighbour takes over; the last stays), move, setBars, the song's appendToSong / insertIntoSong / removeFromSong / moveInSong / setRepeats; at most 16 sections, 16 repeats. It knows no machine: Sections::Host (the studio) captures, applies and measures Machine::sectionContent() and hears the section's length (applyMachines(machines, bars) with a switch, cued with the clips; setSpan(bars) when the one on screen changes length in place: what ONCE plays), so it is unit-tested alone (tests/tst_sections.cpp). Saved in the project (sections); a project without it opens as one section, "A". ravving.sections logs the capture and switch times; SONG mode: songStarting() before the transport, transportStarted/Stopped(), barStarted(bar) (the studio's bar count) cue each entry's section as the previous entry's last bar begins and ask the Host to stopAtBarEnd() at the song's end; playFrom(entry); playingEntry, songBar, entryBar; a QML type (uncreatable) for Sections.Loop / Sections.Song; the exporter's SONG puts the mode on for the take new
    DrumKit reads a soundfont (SF2/SF3 preset → instrument → sample name per key) and finds eight roles: KICK SNARE CLAP TOM HAT OPEN CYMBAL PERC; a role the kit lacks is "—" and silent new
    SketchpadTrack the track object a PatternModel reads (zlChannel): trackType ("synth", or "sample-trig" for the sampler), chainedSounds, muted, ten samples (null, or objects with cppObjId = a clip's id: setSamples())… sketchpad_channel.py
    Sketchpad PlayGridManager's zlSketchpad: SyncTimer's start/stop hooks; launches each track's clip (a or b, trackClip()) on every play; SECTIONS: cueTrackClip() has a track's two clips change places on the next bar (PlayfieldManager applies a bar's changes on its first tick, before the patterns schedule it: nothing drops or doubles), clipPlaying() asks the playfield zynthian_gui_sketchpad.py, playScene
    SoundEngine, FluidSynthEngine an engine process with MIDI in and stereo out; FluidSynth runs a soundfont preset zynthian_engine(_fluidsynth).py
    ConsoleProcess a helper process with a text console, run by libzynthbox's ProcessWrapper (created through the bridge's QML engine): pty, dies with ravving, crash restarts, startup commands, prompt-ended transactions zynthian_engine.py's process handling
    EffectChain the one definition of the SOUND EFFECTS chain: DRIVE COMPRESSOR REVERB EQ PAN SIDECHAIN DELAY, their knobs and defaults (studio.effectChain) new
    TrackEffects a machine's effects (machine.effects) on its track's JackPassthrough: EQ (low shelf 250 Hz / peak 1 kHz / high shelf 5 kHz, ±12 dB), COMPRESSOR, SIDECHAIN (the compressor keyed from DRUMS 1), PAN (= the strip's pan), REVERB/DELAY sends after the fader (setSendLevel(): the machine's VOL gain scales them, so VOL 0 silences the echoes too; the lane itself sends before AudioLevels' gain), DRIVE (the machine's own Calf Saturator, inserted between engine and track only while on: AMOUNT = input gain up to +36 dB + saturation, output 1/(1+1.1·AMOUNT); TONE = post low-pass 1.2 kHz → open); saved with the machine zynthbox-qml's track mixer EQ/compressor
    LiveFx the header's LIVE FX (studio.liveFx): NO KICK / ONLY KICK (drum kick mode, as velocities), LO PASS / HI PASS (sweeps on GlobalPlayback's equaliser), DROP (kick out + high-pass build while held), STUTTER / LOOP (BeatRepeat), CRUSH (Calf Crusher) and PHASER (Calf Phaser) as master inserts GlobalPlayback → repeat → crusher → phaser → speakers, ECHO (every machine thrown into the delay), HEAVY (GlobalPlayback's compressor); taps latch NO/ONLY KICK and the filters, DROP and CRUSH act while held and latch after half a second, STUTTER and LOOP only while held (dragging a held pad picks CRUSH's 8 · 6 · 4 · 3 BIT, STUTTER's 1/16 · 1/32 · 1/64, or LOOP's 1/2 BAR · 1 BEAT · 2 STEPS · 1 STEP, shown on the pad: readouts) new
    DeviceControls the control center's device side (device): screen brightness (the backlight in /sys/class/backlight, 5 % a knob detent as Plasma's applet, never under 30 %, where the screen looks dead; a fixed pot spans 30–100 %), the output jacks' level (the LM4811 through zyncoder, ZynthboxBridge::setOutputLevel), both kept in QSettings ravving/device and put back at start; Wi-Fi (device.wifi, below); Bluetooth power (bluetoothctl, only while the service runs); the address shown (Wi-Fi's, else any network's); CPU, temperature, free storage; reboot / power off. Polled every 2 s only while the drawer is open; commands run through runCommand (command.h: asynchronous, 4 s timeout) zynthbox-qml's settings pages
    Wifi, WifiNetworks, WpaConfig Wi-Fi after Plasma's network applet (plasma-nm's Handler, NetworkModel and password row) on what Zynthbox runs: on / off through Zynthbox's user services (set_wifi.sh), the mode kept as ZYNTHIAN_WIFI_MODE and put back at start (Zynthbox's boot-time wifi-setup.service has wpa_supplicant killed with its oneshot cgroup); the networks in range from wpa_cli scan_results (one row per name, sorted in use / saved / signal, frozen while a row is open); joins saved to Zynthbox's wpa_supplicant.conf (blocks wpa_supplicant would refuse are dropped: one psk="" stops Wi-Fi from starting at all), then reconfigure + select_network; a join that has not taken in 25 s fails (wrong password) and stops retrying. The UI is WifiPopup (password on the row, the on-screen keyboard from Keyboard.qml) zynthbox-qml's Wi-Fi settings (zynconf start_wifi / stop_wifi, its wifi_settings page)
    Paths every folder ravving writes to, decided once at start (Paths::initialise()): config, state (the session), cache, and the library (/zynthian/zynthian-my-data/ravving on the device: projects, presets, samples with takes, recordings); nothing in zynthbox-qml's folders; moves an older layout's files and updates the paths in the session and projects new
    Projects SAVE and PROJECT ▾ (studio.projects): named JSON snapshots of the session (Studio::projectState()) in Paths::projects() (/zynthian/zynthian-my-data/ravving/projects on the device; "RAVE 001"… or the name typed: saveAs tidies it and keeps it unique); save / save as new / open / new / remove, dirty when the session differs from what was saved or opened (compact JSON without the page on screen, checked 300 ms after Studio::modified; at start the session file is what was saved); the current project and dirty flag survive restarts (.current) new
    Exporter EXPORT (studio.exporter, the header's PROJECT ▾ and ExportPopup): a TRACK, the production recorded as the speakers hear it (AudioLevels' GlobalPlayback writer, post master gain) while the transport plays the project from its first beat for BARS × LOOPS bars plus a TAIL; the recording is placed on the audio clock (LiveFx::audioFrame(), the beat repeat's JACK client: SyncTimer's own playhead values are on another clock) a moment before playback, cut from the frame SyncTimer began playback at (ZynthboxBridge::playbackStartFrame) to exactly the bars at the tempo, the tail faded, pulled down to −0.1 dBFS only if it peaked over full scale, and written by libsndfile as WAV 24-bit (default), FLAC, OGG Vorbis or MP3 (AudioFile::writeCut), tagged by TagLib (ID3v2 / Vorbis comments, RIFF INFO too for WAV: title, artist, album, genre, composer, comment, date, BPM, the cover as the picture) with a PNG cover and a JSON sidecar (project, tempo, key, length, peak) beside it. A BUNDLE: .ravv, a zip (ZipWriter) of project.json (the state, every sample it refers to that is not the image's rewritten to samples/), the samples, metadata.json and cover.png; importProject() unpacks one (samples into samples/imported/<name>/) into a new project (Projects::addProject). Metadata as a map or JSON text ({name, format, title, artist, album, genre, composer, comment, bars, loops, tail}); busy / phase / progress, started / finished / failed new
    AlbumArt the cover drawn for a track: a rounded card on a dark sleeve with a gradient from the title and artist (the same name, the same art), soft lights, the big-knob ring, the title, artist and album, ravving's mark with MADE WITH RAVVING; AlbumArtProvider serves it to QML as image://albumart/<title>%09<artist>%09<album> for the dialog's preview new
    ZipWriter, ZipReader a plain zip without a zip library (none with headers on the image): text entries deflated with zlib, samples stored and streamed; stored and deflated entries read back new
    Diagnostics the issues center (issues): {id, component, severity error/warning/notice, title, detail, suggestion, action, since, count}, worst first; Issues::report / Issues::clear from any component (any thread: queued); dismiss hides one until its detail changes; act runs restart / unset-gdb / reboot; log keeps the last warnings (the message handler in main feeds it); reported → a toast new
    StderrWatch fd 2 made a pipe early in main, teed to the real stderr by a thread that scans each line: zyncoder's "Error while processing GPIO events!" (the keys died) and a few more become issues; nothing else can see those lines new
    AudioGraph the graph's clock (sample rate and period), read once through a throwaway JACK client (48000 / 256 without one); FluidSynth is started with it (-r, audio.period-size, audio.periods=2) so its PipeWire stream runs in step with the graph instead of at 44100 / 1024 resampled (routing.md) new
    AudioOutputs where the sound goes (outputs, the control center's AUDIO OUTPUT card): PipeWire's sinks from pw-dump ({name, description, kind jacks / hdmi / bluetooth / other, address}); the jacks stay the graph's clock with the master wired to system:playback_*; any other sink is fed through a pw-loopback of ravving's own (a virtual sink ravving-out whose playback stream targets the chosen sink at a 2048-frame latency), the master's output ports linked to it with pw-link (its inputs have no JACK name; the link is verified with pw-link -l and retried until the ports exist) and the jacks muted (wpctl set-mute), not unlinked, so they keep the clock; the Bluetooth sink is never made the default sink, which would clock the JACK graph by the Bluetooth transport (glitches) and leave the jack links in place (sound from both) new
    OutputWatch ravving-outwatch, a JACK client of two inputs that listens, while another output plays, to what the master sends into AudioOutputs' loopback and what the loopback plays out (peaks kept on the audio thread, compared every second): music in with nothing out for 6 s says silent() (AudioOutputs routes again once, then reports "… plays no sound"), sound out again flowing(); idle (the jacks) it is disconnected new
    Bluetooth Bluetooth (bluetooth, on KDE's BluezQt): the adapter (state unavailable / off / switching / on with reason, power, discovery, discoverable for three minutes), the devices ({address, name, kind phone / computer / audio / input / other, paired, connected, trusted, rssi, audio = A2DP sink, receives = OBEX push}; nameless private addresses hidden unless paired), a pairing Agent (DisplayYesNo: pairing {kind confirm / pin / passkey / display, passkey}, answerPairing), pair → trust → connect, forget, OBEX Object Push sending (sendFile: a session per push, transfer {name, device, size, transferred, status}), an ObexAgent that accepts pushes into library/incoming/ (fileReceived), and useAsOutput: the device's sink found among AudioOutputs by address (asked again until it appears after the connection) and selected there, back to the jacks on disconnect, forget or exit new
    RemovableDrives USB sticks (drives): lsblk -J every two seconds for USB partitions with a file system ({device, disk, label, size, fsType, mountPoint, mounted}), mount() through udisksctl mount -b (synchronous, lands under /media/root/ new
    UsbLibrary the USB page's backend (usb): scan() mounts the stick and walks it in a worker (QtConcurrent), sorting files by kind (samples, soundfonts, projects, tracks under ravving/exports, MIDI files; hidden / System Volume Information / FOUND.* / $RECYCLE.BIN skipped, 20 000 files at most); entries(kind) with onDevice, deviceEntries(kind) with onStick; importEntry / exportEntry copy in a worker through a .part file with progress (fsync onto the stick), projects through the Exporter's bundle import / exportProjectFile; finished(kind, name, ok, message) new
    KeyedListModel the long lists' model (QML type): a new items list applied as the fewest row changes by a key field; roles = the fields seen (only grow), modelData the row; tests in tests/ new

    libzynthbox's contracts

    Break one and libzynthbox crashes or stays silent:

    • SketchpadTrack.samples must hold ten entries (PatternModel reads sample slots 0–9).
    • SamplerSynth makes a track's channel_N-sampleM ports only while a clip is registered on that slot: wire exactly the loaded ones, again whenever they change.
    • AudioLevels starts every recorder set to record: a take turns the master's writer off (setRecordGlobalPlayback(false)), the mix turns the port recorder off.
    • AudioLevels' own capture meter (captureA/B) reads the speakers under PipeWire (it takes the first system: outputs, the monitor): meter the input through the port recorder.
    • PipeWire has a second "Built-in Audio Stereo" (the Dummy card): its ports get a number suffix (capture_FL-17167); the plain names are the HiFiBerry's.
    • PlayGridManager's zlSketchpad must be set before playing (SyncTimer dereferences it).
    • A pattern only plays while PlayfieldManager has its clip in PlayingState, and stopping resets every clip: the clips are re-launched from Sketchpad::startPlayback().
    • Never call Plugin::shutdown(); exit with _exit() (see the exit row below).

    Kits

    data/kits.json holds every machine:

    • drumMachines: track, Zynthian channel, engine, default kit and preset.
    • machines, by kind: mono (bass on track 3, lead on track 5) and chords (track 4). Each has its engine (type, LV2 URI, fixed controls), its sound (SOUND MODIFIERS), a presets file, defaultPreset, baseNote and velocities.
    • The kit folder: the 40 drum-machine soundfonts in zynthian-data.

    Studio builds the machines generically and publishes the non-drum ones as studio.machines.<key>. A new engine type is one SoundEngine subclass plus a line in SoundEngine::createEngine().

    Drum kits

    Grid rows aren't written by hand: DrumKit reads them out of the kit, since the soundfonts aren't General MIDI (the 909's clap is on 50, the Linn's on 39).

    • A tap on a machine's nameplate opens ChoicePopup with every kit.
    • DrumMachine::loadKit() moves each hit to the new kit's note for its role, keeping accents. The engine reloads the soundfont (SoundEngine::reconfigure), and the session keeps the choice (session.json).
    • A double tap on the nameplate goes back to the default kit (resetKit()).

    Presets

    Factory beats that replace the pattern: House, Techno, Electro, Breaks, Hip Hop, Groove and Empty. They're drum charts written per role ("KICK": "X...x...", X = an accented hit), so every preset plays on every kit.

    • The toolbar's PRESET button shows the loaded preset (• once edited) and opens a ChoicePopup by genre.
    • SAVE CURRENT AS NEW stores the pattern as "My Beat N" in Paths::presets()/drum-presets.json, and DELETE removes a user preset.
    • The other machines save the same way into their own file, named by the factory file's userName: "My Bassline N", "My Progression N", "My Melody N", "My Pattern N".

    Row mix

    Each row's level, mute and solo are kept per role, so they survive kit changes. They live in the hits' velocities:

    • level 0.8 → velocity 100, and an accent hits ×1.27 harder;
    • a muted or soloed-out row gets velocity −1, PatternModel's "do not play".

    The machine keeps the accents and saves them in the session with the row mix. They're never read back from velocities.

    The session lives in Paths::state() (~/.local/state/ravving; an explicit path: libzynthbox's MidiRouter renames the application to "zynthbox-qml" while it initialises).

    The pattern interface. StepGrid and PatternToolbar only draw and forward to a pattern object: rows, stepCount, steps, accents, playhead, bars, viewBar, presets, presetName, presetEdited, canSavePresets, rowLevels, rowMuted, rowSoloed, toggleStep(), toggleAccent(), clear(), loadPreset(), saveAsPreset(), removePreset(), setRowLevel(), setRowMuted(), setRowSoloed(), audition(). The studio's DrumMachine and SamplerMachine implement it; PatternState.qml implements it in memory for pages whose backend is still to come (the melodic toolbars).
