    Load a preset

    Pick a preset from ravving or a USB stick, choose what comes along, and undo it.

    A preset is a ready-made pattern for one instrument, sometimes with the sound it was written for. Here's how to load one, from your MIKO, from a USB stick or online.

    Open the presets

    1. Open the instrument's page: 1 to 5.
    2. Tap the preset's name in the pattern toolbar, the box beside LOCK.

    The PRESETS dialog opens on the source your current pattern came from, with that preset marked.

    Pick where it comes from

    SOURCE shows DEVICE, ONLINE, plus one chip for each USB stick plugged in. Pick a stick to play its presets straight from it, without copying anything.

    ONLINE lists every preset in the online catalog for this instrument, from the single patterns and from inside every pack. Tap one and it plays right away: only that preset comes down, and nothing gets saved.

    Like it? SAVE TO DEVICE keeps it with your own. Offline, you still see the last list, and the ones you've played before still load.

    Choose what comes along

    The three LOAD switches decide what a preset brings:

    Switch On Off
    WITH ITS SOUND the instrument switches to the preset's sound your sound stays
    WITH ITS MODS the preset's MODS are set your MODS stay
    WITH ITS BARS the preset's own length laid over your bars: a shorter preset repeats, a longer one is cut

    Turning on WITH ITS SOUND turns on WITH ITS MODS too, since a sound is heard with its MODS. You can switch MODS off again. Your choices are remembered.

    A preset that carries a sound shows ♪ and the sound's name under its genre, plus "+ MODS" when it has MODS. Without a sound, WITH ITS SOUND is dimmed while that preset is highlighted, and WITH ITS MODS is dimmed without MODS.

    Find one and load it

    • GROUP: a chip per genre, or ALL.
    • SORT: AS LISTED or A–Z.
    • ★: hold a preset to make it a favourite.

    Tap a preset to load it. The dialog closes, and the toolbar shows the preset's name.

    Changed your mind?

    Tap ↶ in the toolbar, or press BACK on the grid. One undo puts your pattern back.

    If the preset brought its own sound, that undo puts your old sound back too. Only the sound's knobs (MODS) aren't part of the undo history.

    Every pack brings its own groove. Try a few, then undo the ones you don't like.
