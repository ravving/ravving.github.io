    Sections and the song

    The design and the decisions behind SECTIONS, the queue, SONG mode, and what each phase measured.

    A song is more than one loop. A section is every machine's pattern at once (the intro, the verse, the bridge); the song is the sections in order, each played a number of times. This page is the design, decided before the code (TODO 9b), and the phases it is built in, each closed by its review.

    What easyrave does, and what ravving does better

    easyrave's SECTIONS (+) (site/index.html, "SONG SECTIONS") is a row of numbered chips: ＋ saves the whole song (every machine with its sounds, tempo, swing, key, the live FX, mutes, master) as a section; a click recalls it at once, a double click renames, × deletes, a drag reorders. Three things it does not do, which ravving should:

    easyrave ravving
    a recall lands at once, wherever the bar is it lands on the next bar line, sample-accurately, as Zynthbox launches clips
    sections are snapshots: edits after a recall are lost unless saved again as a new section sections are live: what you edit is the section on screen, kept when you move on
    no song: switching is by hand a song: the sections in order with repeats, played through (SONG) or one looped (LOOP), exported in one take

    Decided (the user, 2026-10-09)

    A section holds the patterns and what plays (not sounds, kits, MODS, effects or levels); sections are live (edits stay in the section on screen); a section picked while playing takes over on the next bar; the sections live on the header's plate and a SONG page (no strip taking height from every page).

    The parts

    What a section holds

    The patterns and what plays, per machine: the steps, lines and chords, the bars, accents, slides, note lengths, live timing, automation lanes, the drums' row mix (levels, mutes, solos) and ❚❚ (a machine resting in the intro). That is Machine::contentState(), what undo already keeps, less what changes the engine: the kit and the sound source.

    Sounds, MODS, effects, levels, tempo, swing and key stay the project's. A kit or a source change restarts FluidSynth or jalv (0.3 s and more of silence): at a section change it would be a gap in the music, which "audio first" rules out.

    Switching on the bar line: two clips per track

    Every sketchpad track has five clips; ravving plays clip a only. Each machine gets a second, clip b, and the two take turns:

    1. Cue: the section to come is written into the clip that is not playing (the machine's model takes the new content and renders it there, applyContent()); the playing clip is not touched, so nothing in it can drop or double.
    2. Switch: PlayfieldManager is told, for the next bar, to start that clip and stop the other (setClipPlaystate(…, NextBarPosition)), which libzynthbox does on the audio thread at the bar's first tick, the way Zynthbox launches clips.
    3. A machine whose content is the same in both sections is not switched: it keeps playing (a five-bar brush pattern carries on across the change instead of restarting).

    When: a section picked by hand while playing is cued at once and switches at the next bar (the grids show it at once, the chip blinks until it plays); in SONG, the next section is cued as the last bar of the current one begins (a whole bar of margin for the main thread). Stopped, a pick is immediate (nothing plays).

    The bar count comes from SyncTimer's 16th beats since the start (Studio already follows them); Sketchpad's start launches, per track, the clip that holds the section, not always clip a.

    Song and modes

    • LOOP (the default, today's behaviour): the current section loops; picking another switches at the next bar.
    • SONG: the song plays from its first entry (or the one picked) to its end, each entry its section × repeats; at the end it stops, or loops the song (a switch).
    • A section's length is its longest pattern (8 bars at most), shown and changeable (1–8).
    • A song entry: a section and repeats (×1…×8). A section may appear more than once (A A B A).

    Editing

    The section on screen is the one edited; its content is kept in the section when another is picked (and with the project). New sections start as a copy of the current one (DUPLICATE) or empty (NEW): a song is mostly variations. Undo stays per machine and starts over on a section change (it would otherwise undo another section's edit unseen).

    Saving

    The project gets sections: {list: [{id, name, colour, bars, machines: {key: content}}], song: [{section, repeats}], current, mode}. A project without it opens as one section, "A", holding what it has: nothing changes for the projects saved so far. The .ravv bundle carries it unchanged. Presets are untouched: a machine's preset still loads into the section on screen.

    Export

    EXPORT → TRACK gains SONG (the default when the song has more than one entry): the whole song in one take, its length from the song, plus the tail. LOOP keeps today's BARS × LOOPS.

    The interface

    • Header: the project plate reads the section too ("Autumn Leaves · B 3/4"); a tap opens the SONG page. While a section is cued its name blinks there.
    • SONG page (full screen, like the music player): the sections as cards (name, colour, length, which machines play, ⧉ duplicate, rename, delete) with ＋; the song as a strip of blocks, each as wide as its bars × repeats, the playing one with a moving playhead, tap to play from there, drag to reorder, the repeats on a stepper; LOOP / SONG; a tap on a card picks that section.
    • Hardware: ALT + step key N picks section N (the step keys light in the sections' colours while ALT is held, the playing one bright, a cued one blinking); on the SONG page the knobs and arrows walk the cards and the strip.
    • Each section has a colour (from a fixed palette, in order), the same on the plate, the cards, the strip and the LEDs.

    Status

    Phase 1, the model: done (2026-10-09). Sections (src/backend/sections.h) with its Host, the studio; Machine::sectionContent() / applySectionContent() and the samplers' SamplerBanks; saved in the project, old projects open as section "A"; 18 unit tests (tests/tst_sections.cpp, they found a name cut leaving a trailing space). On milo: Autumn Leaves rebuilt as A1 A2 B C SOLO from its presets, the song A1 A2 B C SOLO A1 A2 B C (72 bars), each pick bringing back its own lines (A2's held E♭5, C's E♭ E♭ C C, the solo), no sample loaded by a switch, saved and opened again unchanged (not dirty). A capture takes 2–3 ms, a switch (every machine's pattern rewritten) about 60 ms, on the main thread. Startup: restoring the sections takes 19 ms; an A/B against the build before today (6fda638) showed the same 7.9–8.1 s to wired on the same project and page, so nothing measurable is added (the gap to the 5.1 s of quality.md §2 is the device's state, not this code). Review: clang-tidy at the baseline, clazy's three new range-loop detaches and the Qt linters' two findings in the new files fixed. Until phase 2 a pick applies at once, wherever the bar is.

    Phase 2, on the bar line: done (2026-10-09). Read in libzynthbox first: each tick PlayGridManager advances the playfield (a bar's clip changes, all on its first tick) before the patterns schedule that tick, with no look-ahead (sequenceProgressionLength 0), so a clip started and one stopped on the same bar meet exactly. Each machine has its track's clips a and b (Machine::attachSpare(), the PAD SAMPLER's second track too); a pick while playing writes the section into the clip not playing (cueSectionContent()), or into the one already waiting when picked again, and Sketchpad::cueTrackClip() has them change places on the next bar; a machine whose part does not change is left playing; ❚❚ in a section goes with the bar too (no note cut at the pick). Sections shows switching until the studio sees, at a bar's start, the clips changed (asked of the playfield, not counted). Measured on milo (tools/sections-test.sh): 146 eighths through 24 random picks, none missing or doubled, 10 changes, every one on a bar line, rests in whole bars; tools/audio-impact.sh playing, with a pick every 1.5 s against none: 0 xruns both, realtime CPU 15 % both, GUI 10 % against 9 %. The one edge: a hit recorded early (Q off) on the first step of the section to come cannot sound, its clip not playing yet when it is due.

    Phase 3, the interface: done (2026-10-09). Two changes to the plan above, with the user: the SONG page sits under the header, as the MIXER does, rather than over the whole screen (the transport, the tempo and the plate stay at hand while arranging), and the sections follow easyrave's SECTIONS shelf (the user's reference: square keys wearing their number, the name under, ＋ saving the one on screen again, tap / double tap / × / drag), at a finger's size since the header has no room for easyrave's 19–28 px keys; ALT + step keys 9–16 pick sections 1–8 (ALT + 1–8 are the LIVE FX already). A copy now takes its own colour (COLOUR changes it), the song's blocks being told apart by it. Checked on milo by touch: the plate opens it, a tap picks, a double tap renames, ＋ copies, × × deletes, a drag moves (5 before 2 and back), ALT + step 11 picks B, a pick while playing blinks until its bar (switching true, then false with the section playing); idle it draws 0 frames/s. Review: the Qt QML linter (one clip on the strip's ListView, which scrolls), qmllint, static-analysis.sh handlers, clang-tidy on the C++ (none); 21 unit tests.

    Phase 4, SONG mode and the song's export: done (2026-10-09). Sections follows the song bar by bar (barStarted(), the bars since the start counted by the studio: libzynthbox's 16th beat counts within the bar): as an entry's last bar begins, the next entry's section is cued through phase 2's path (the same section: it plays on, nothing is touched); as the song's last bar begins the studio stops every clip on the next bar (Sketchpad:: stopAllAtNextBar()) and then the transport. PLAY in SONG mode puts the first entry's section in place before the first note (songStarting()); PLAY FROM HERE starts any entry (from the next bar while playing); a section picked by hand jumps to its first entry (none: LOOP); song edits while it plays keep the entry playing (removing it: LOOP). The SONG page's LOOP | SONG, the block playing with its progress, "PLAYING PART 6, BAR 44"; the plate's "6/9". EXPORT → WHAT: SONG (its length, once; up to 2048 bars) or LOOP (BARS × LOOPS); the export puts SONG mode on and back. Measured on milo: from part 9 the song stopped on the bar after its 8th; C → SOLO → A1 cued on each 8th bar (27 and 9 ms); Autumn Leaves exported in one take, 72 bars + 4 s tail = 122.36 s, its sections' levels within 0.4 dB of the hand-joined WAV, silent after the tail; audio-impact playing in SONG against LOOP on the same project: the same CPU, 0 xruns. 28 unit tests (the song through, the same section twice, a pick and PLAY FROM while playing, SONG taking over, edits while playing, a missed bar). Sections is a QML type now (its enum by name; uncreatable). zb note: libzynthbox's metronomeBeat16th counts within the bar.

    Phases, each closed by a review

    1. The model (C++ Sections, no audio yet): sections, song, current, mode; capture and apply through Machine::contentState() / applyContent() minus the engine keys; saving and the migration; unit tests (tests/, QAbstractItemModelTester, the song's bar math, old projects). Review: qt-cpp-review, clang-tidy / clazy clean, the API documented.
    2. On the bar line: clip b per track, cue and switch through PlayfieldManager, Sketchpad launching the right clip, unchanged machines left alone. Measured on milo: a click on every 16th of section A and a different pitch in B, recorded across a hundred switches (by hand and in SONG): no note missing, none doubled, none late (onsets against the grid); tools/audio-impact.sh while switching: no xruns, no realtime page faults added.
    3. The interface: the plate, the SONG page, ALT + step keys and LEDs. Review: qt-qml-review and qmllint, the screenshot tour at 1280×800, the delegates on KeyedListModel, device.sh animations: nothing drawing while idle.
    4. SONG and export: playing the song through, the export of the whole song, the Autumn Leaves project rebuilt as A1 A2 B C + the solo chorus and exported in one take (against the hand-joined WAV).
    5. Docs and the quality pass: architecture.md, scripting-projects.md (the fifo calls), the user-facing help; the full static-analysis run, QML profiler on the SONG page, boot timing unchanged.

    Each phase is deployed on milo, tested there, committed on its own.

    Risks

    • libzynthbox's NextBarPosition with two clips of one track: Zynthbox uses it per clip; that a stop and a start on one track land on the same tick is to be measured in phase 2 before anything is built on it (a click track recorded across the switch). The fallback, if it slips: both clips change on the bar through one setClipPlaystate pair at CurrentPosition with the bar's tick as the offset.
    • The PAD SAMPLER has a second track (pads 11–16) and SAMPLE KING plays samples: their sections hold the hits, not the samples (loading samples is not for the bar line).
    • Picking a section marked the project changed (fixed 2026-10-09). Projects::comparable leaves out sections.current and, on every machine, the keys the section on screen holds of it (its patterns and rest, repeated at the machine's top level). Nothing real hides: the sections' list captures the section on screen live, so an edit to it (a step, a sample in a pad) still changes the list. Checked on milo: the song played across a section change, a section picked by hand → unchanged; a step added → changed; taken out again → unchanged.
    • Automation recorded in one section stays in it; a live knob sweep across a switch keeps going (it is the knob, not the section).
