    Film your session

    LIVE REC: a video of the screen or a camera with the sound in sync, or the sound alone, with your voice over it.

    Record what you play as a video: the screen, or a USB camera, with the sound in sync. Or record the sound alone. Starting a recording doesn't stop the music.

    Start LIVE REC

    1. Press MENU and tap LIVE REC.
    2. Choose WHAT to record:
      • VIDEO: the screen or a USB camera, with the sound, as an MP4 among your VIDEOS;
      • AUDIO ONLY: just the sound, as a WAV among your TRACKS.
    3. Choose whether the MIC is in, and what your voice is: VOICE-OVER (the music ducks while you talk) or SINGING (the music stays).
    4. Tap START.

    The dialog is the confirmation, so a stray tap never starts a recording.

    While it records

    The plate counts down, REC IN 3, then shows the time: ● REC 00:04. The menu closes first, so it isn't in the picture.

    On a video, every key you press is named at the bottom of the screen, so viewers can see what you played.

    Stop it

    Press STOP. It ends the recording, and the music with it.

    LIVE REC stays on afterwards, ready: press RECORD to start another take, from any page. To switch it off, press STOP again within three seconds (a notice reminds you), or tap the LIVE REC card in the menu again.

    When it ends

    The VIDEO SAVED dialog shows the file, how long it is, and its size.

    • DELETE: tap it twice within three seconds.
    • RENAME: give it a name of your own.
    • SEND…: to a USB stick, one of your computers, or your phone.

    An audio recording also has METADATA…: tag it, draw its cover, and save it as WAV, FLAC, OGG or MP3, like an exported track.

    Sing along! With the MIC in, the music ducks under your voice.

    Talk over the music, or sing

    With the MIC in and VOICE-OVER picked, the music ducks while you talk, and comes back when you stop; your voice comes out dry and close. With SINGING, the music stays and your voice gets a little room, an echo on the beat and a plate. If MIC TOO HOT shows up, turn the GAIN down in SETTINGS → LIVE REC.

    While LIVE REC is on with the MIC in, the input is the recording's alone. The RECORDER page and the samplers' INPUT wait until you leave LIVE REC, and nothing you set on the RECORDER page touches the recording.

    Settings

    MENU → SETTINGS → LIVE REC has the QUALITY, the TIMER before a recording starts and the SOURCE (the screen or a USB camera). It also has the recording's own mic: its INPUT and GAIN, the VOICE (VOICE-OVER or SINGING), its EFFECTS, and how far the music ducks. They're kept for next time.
