    Using ravving / guide/film-a-session.md

    Film your session

    LIVE REC: a video of the screen or a camera with the sound in sync, or the sound alone, with your voice over it.

    Record what you play as a video: the screen, or a USB camera, with the sound in sync. Or record the sound alone. Either way, the music keeps playing.

    Start LIVE REC

    1. Press MENU and tap LIVE REC.
    2. Choose WHAT to record:
      • VIDEO: the screen or a USB camera, with the sound, as an MP4 among your VIDEOS;
      • AUDIO ONLY: just the sound, as a WAV among your TRACKS.
    3. Choose whether the MIC is in. With it on, your voice goes in over the music.
    4. Tap START.

    The dialog is the confirmation, so a stray tap never starts a recording.

    While it records

    The plate counts down, REC IN 3, then shows the time: ● REC 00:04. The menu closes first, so it isn't in the picture.

    On a video, every key you press is named at the bottom of the screen, so viewers can see what you played.

    Stop it

    Press STOP. It ends the recording, and the music with it.

    LIVE REC stays on afterwards, ready: press RECORD to start another take, from any page. To switch it off, tap the LIVE REC card in the menu again.

    When it ends

    The VIDEO SAVED dialog shows the file, how long it is, and its size.

    • DELETE: tap it twice within three seconds.
    • RENAME: give it a name of your own.
    • SEND…: to a USB stick, one of your computers, or your phone.

    An audio recording also has METADATA…: tag it, draw its cover, and save it as WAV, FLAC, OGG or MP3, like an exported track.

    Sing along! With the MIC in, the music ducks under your voice.

    Talk over the music

    With the MIC in, the music ducks while you talk, and comes back when you stop. If MIC TOO HOT shows up, turn the RECORDER's GAIN down.

    Settings

    MENU → SETTINGS → VIDEO has the QUALITY, the TIMER before a recording starts, the SOURCE (the screen or a USB camera), and the voice-over mix.
