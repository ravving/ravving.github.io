    The machines

    Every instrument page and the engines and sounds it can play, all of them open source.

    Each number key opens an instrument page. Every page starts with its own engine, and you can swap in another sound from the nameplate. Here's what each one can play.

    Drums: key 1

    Three drum machines, DRUMS 1, 2 and 3. Press 1 again to move between them.

    • Engine: FluidSynth, playing soundfont drum kits.
    • To start: a Roland TR909, a TR808 and a LINN9000.
    • Classic drum machines: forty kits, from the Roland CR78, TR606 and TR727 to the SP1200, Simmons, Alesis HR16 and Yamaha RX11.
    • Acoustic kits: Jazz, Brush, Standard, Room, Power and Orchestra, plus cumbia, Latin, folk and rock kits.

    Chords: key 2

    • Engine: TAL NoiseMaker, a virtual analog synth, with its factory sounds. Zynthbox calls it Noize Mak3r.
    • Or a soundfont: about fifty sounds. Pianos, Rhodes, Wurlitzer, organs, string sections, guitars, pads, and a set of strange "alien" textures.

    Bass: key 3

    • Engine: Nekobi, a 303-style bass synth.
    • Or: Calf Monosynth as an 808 sub bass.
    • Or a soundfont: upright, acoustic, finger, fretless, picked, slap and synth basses, a sine sub, contrabass, cello, and an organ's low pedal.

    Lead: key 4

    • Engine: Calf Monosynth, a monophonic synth.
    • Or a soundfont: about forty sounds. Saxes, trumpets, flute, oboe, violin and cello, guitars clean and heavy, harp, celesta, music box, vibes, marimba and more.

    Samplers: key 5

    Key 5 opens SAMPLE KING or the PAD SAMPLER. Press it again to switch.

    • Engine: libzynthbox's own sampler, playing your samples.
    • SAMPLE KING: four banks of eight slots, to trim, pitch, reverse and chop.
    • PAD SAMPLER: sixteen pads on the step keys, MPC-style.

    The effects

    Every page has a SOUND EFFECTS chain, and the header has the LIVE FX.

    Effect Engine
    DRIVE Calf Saturator
    COMPRESSOR, EQ, PAN, SIDECHAIN libzynthbox's mixer
    REVERB Dragonfly Room Reverb
    DELAY TAP Echo
    CRUSH, PHASER (LIVE FX) Calf Crusher, Calf Phaser
    STUTTER, LOOP (LIVE FX) ravving's own

    All open source

    Every engine here is open source, and all of them ship with Zynthbox. ravving doesn't bundle any of them: it starts them on your MIKO as it needs them.
