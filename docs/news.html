    What's new

    Every release, newest first: what it adds and what it fixes, each linked to where it's explained.

    Every ravving release, newest first: what you can do now that you couldn't before, what works better, and where to read more. To get the newest version, see Update it.

    ravving 0.7

    Plug in your controller: it plays along, lights and all.

    November 16, 2026. Bring your own gear: keyboards, pad controllers and mixers you plug in now play ravving, and your beats can loosen up and groove like a real player.

    New

    • Grooves that breathe. Tap FEEL on any instrument and turn its dials. The drums play a touch early or late and softer or harder, the chords strum, and bass and lead notes get shorter, a little differently every time round. FEEL
    • Ghost notes. Pick some drum hits and turn GHOST on: they play very softly, for that in-between feel. Shape a drum hit
    • Play along with a solo. Solo one instrument and play another one LIVE: you hear what you play, while that instrument's pattern stays quiet. The MIXER
    • Drums that ring. Every drum hit rings out to its end, like on a drum machine, and a closed hi-hat cuts an open one short. Shape a drum hit

    Playing live

    • Jump around while playing. ALT + X goes a bar back and ALT + Y a bar on, without stopping. Press quickly to jump several bars. Skip a bar
    • PLAY and STOP like a music player. ▶ pauses where you are, ■ goes back to the start.
    • Tap the tempo. Tap the BPM display, or ALT + METRONOME, in time with the music. Tempo and swing
    • Switch sections on the beat. With SWITCH · BEAT, a section you pick comes in on the next beat, not the next bar. When a switch lands
    • Light shows. Set how bright the key lights are, and enjoy a little show while ravving starts. Key lights

    Your controllers

    • Your MIDI keyboard or pads, your way. Plug one in by USB or Bluetooth and it remembers its own settings: channel, transpose, how loud soft touches play, bend range. MIDI controllers
    • Teach it your keys and knobs. LEARN DRUMS and LEARN A CONTROL give any key, pad or knob a job. Bends, pressure and slides you play are kept in your recordings. MIDI controllers
    • Pad controllers that light up with you. A light-up pad controller shows your key and scale, and plays the drums and chords as pads. Light-up pads
    • A real mixer at your fingertips. A controller with faders becomes ravving's mixer: one fader per instrument, with mute, solo, record and pause, its lights following along. A mixer controller
    • Or build your own mixer. Teach any controller VOLUME, PAN, MUTE, SOLO, REC and PAUSE. Any controller as a mixer
    • Wireless MIDI. Pair a Bluetooth controller and see how much battery it has left. Find and pair a device

    On the screen

    • See what's making sound. A small lamp on every instrument lights up while it plays. The sound lamps
    • Every instrument at a glance. Its name in the header shows whether it's soloed, muted, turned all the way down, paused, recording or played LIVE. The instruments
    • The MIXER in decibels, with a scale beside every fader. The MIXER
    • A start screen that keeps you posted on what ravving is getting ready. Startup splash
    • SETTINGS opens where you left it.
    • Sound pickers that say what they play, so you know which instrument you're choosing for.

    Smoother

    • LIVE FX and pad effects fade in and out instead of switching with a click. LIVE FX
    • Volume, mute and the faders glide, so a quick move never pops.

    Fixed

    • If an instrument goes quiet on its own, ravving brings its sound back and tells you in the issues center. The issues center
    • The issues center no longer warns about notes you silenced on purpose with a mute or a solo.
    • A knob you aren't touching no longer turns a drum row down by itself.
    • Changing a drum kit's TUNE no longer makes the drums go silent.
    • ＋ on a section with a long name no longer freezes the screen.
    • A controller connected two ways at once no longer plays every key twice.
    • Notes from a controller never get stuck, and a muted instrument stays silent, even what you play live on it.
    • A controller can no longer swap your drum kit's sounds by mistake.
    • The samplers no longer miss notes sent from a controller.
    • The sampler pans the way the knob says, and its echo stays on that side.
    • NEW stops recording and playback first, then starts with every instrument unmuted and unsoloed. A new project starts clean
    • When you pause, the playhead stays where you stopped.

    ravving 0.6

    New moves! Every section can bring its own sound now.

    November 2, 2026. Each part of your song can have its own sounds, and the timing is tighter than ever.

    New

    • A sound for each section. Choose THIS SECTION in a sound picker, and that part of the song gets its own sound, MODS included. A sound of its own per section
    • Go back to an earlier version. Hold SAVE to keep a version of your project, then find it again in PROJECT ▾ → TIMELINE. The timeline
    • SELECT on every grid. Pick some hits and give them a CHANCE to play, RATCHETs that repeat them, or the passes they PLAY ON. Then move, copy or delete them together. Shape a drum hit
    • Chords that hold across the voicings that follow, each chord with its own LENGTH, VELOCITY, NUDGE and STRUM. Shape a chord
    • More than one note at a time on the bass and lead, with POLY. POLY
    • Every control of a sound. Hold MODS to reach them all with ALL MODS. Every control of the sound
    • Sounds from anywhere. Browse ONLINE as well as your own sounds and USB sticks, and hear a sound with PREVIEW before you load it. Soundfonts from a stick or online
    • New icons: CANDY, BLOCK and HAIRLINE, or draw your own set. Icons, Make an icon pack

    Fixed

    • Notes and section changes land right on the beat. When a switch lands
    • Recording the whole mix and EXPORT now capture exactly what you hear, LIVE FX included. Record the whole mix
    • A project in another key opens in its own key on the bass and lead.
    • Volume and pan moves glide instead of clicking, and a muted instrument stays silent.
    • A sample that can't be read, or one deleted while it plays, no longer stops the sound.
    • OCT − and + keep the instrument's name on screen.
    • The mixer shows the sound each instrument plays again.
    • A power cut while saving can no longer leave you with half a project.

    ravving 0.5

    Fresh downloads, right on the device.

    October 20, 2026. GET MORE brings new packs, sounds and looks straight to your device.

    New

    • GET MORE: genre packs, patterns, sounds, templates, skins, soundfonts and demo projects to download right on the device, then UPDATE or REMOVE them. GET MORE
    • A mixer strip for each instrument, with a fader, PAN, ❚❚, S and M. The mixer
    • Tidier SETTINGS, sorted into GENERAL, KEYS, LOOK, LIVE FX, LIVE REC, PROFILE, CONNECTIONS and HELP & ABOUT. Settings
    • New skins, with PASTEL NIGHT as the default. Each one is a plain file you can share, or make your own. Skins, Make a skin set
    • Run the music from the device's own keys. Want the buttons on screen back? Turn on TRANSPORT ON SCREEN.
    • Sing or talk over your music. Pick SINGING or VOICE-OVER for the LIVE REC microphone, each with its own effects. Talking over the music
    • Save as a template too, with ALSO AS TEMPLATE, and put your name on every export with AUTHOR. Templates

    ravving 0.4

    Thirty pads to play with!

    October 14, 2026. LIVE FX grows to thirty pads, SETTINGS gets its own page, and ravving speaks Spanish and Japanese.

    New

    • Thirty LIVE FX pads in two banks, from KILL LOW to FREEZE, under ALT and the step keys. LIVE FX, Play it live
    • SETTINGS as a page of its own, and FIXED KNOBS to choose how a knob that can't turn forever picks up a value: PICK UP, JUMP or SCALE. Fixed knobs
    • A fresh look, with each page in its own colours.
    • Spanish and Japanese, in the whole app and on this webpage. Languages
    • LOOP or ONCE for a pattern that's shorter than its section. Loop or once
    • More ways to send your music: to any number of computers, to a phone by QR code, and your whole library on a phone. Browse from your phone
    • New packs: Body, Hollywood, R&B, Droon, Bebop and Solos. The genre packs

    Fixed

    • Bluetooth speakers and headphones get a moment to settle before the sound starts, and the issues center tells you when one stays silent. Audio output
    • A number key on the SONG page, the mixer, the player or the RECORDER takes you back to the page you had open.
    • The fixed knobs pick a value up in the control center too, so nothing jumps.
    • A pad stops playing before you change its sample.
    • The metronome no longer clicks after the effects.
    • WITH ITS SOUND and WITH ITS MODS are greyed out when a preset doesn't have them. Loading a preset

    • SEND… a file to a USB stick, your computer, or a phone by QR code. Send a file to your phone
    • FILES: everything you've made, sorted by kind, to send or delete. Files
    • A new project starts clean on every page. A new project starts clean
    • The Vuelo pack, nu-disco with a template for every key. The genre packs
