    Every release, newest first: what it adds and what it fixes, each linked to where it's explained.

    ravving 0.6

    New moves! Every section can bring its own sound now.

    November 2, 2026. Each section can play its own sounds, SELECT works on every grid, and the timing is tighter than ever.

    New

    • A sound for each section. THIS SECTION in a sound picker gives the section its own sound, MODS included. A sound of its own per section
    • Versions of your project. Hold SAVE to keep one, then go back from PROJECT ▾ → TIMELINE. The timeline
    • SELECT on every grid. Give picked hits a CHANCE, RATCHETs and PLAY ON, then move, copy or delete them together. Shape a drum hit
    • Chords that hold through changing voicings, each with its own LENGTH, VELOCITY, NUDGE and STRUM. Shape a chord
    • POLY on the bass and lead: several notes on one step. POLY
    • ALL MODS: hold MODS to reach every control of a sound. Every control of the sound
    • Sounds from anywhere: ONLINE beside DEVICE and your sticks, and PREVIEW to hear a sound first. Soundfonts from a stick or online
    • Icon sets: CANDY, BLOCK and HAIRLINE, or a pack of your own. Icons, Make an icon pack

    Fixed

    • Notes and section switches land right on the beat: the sequencer now runs inside the audio cycle. When a switch lands
    • ALT + ● and EXPORT record what the speakers play, LIVE FX included. Record the whole mix
    • A project opened after one in another key keeps its own key on the bass and lead.
    • Volume and pan moves glide instead of clicking, and a muted machine stays silent.
    • A sample that can't be read, or one removed while it plays, no longer stops the sound.
    • OCT − and + keep the machine's name on screen.
    • The mixer's plates show the sound each machine plays again.
    • A project file is written whole or not at all, so a power cut can't leave half a project.

    ravving 0.5

    Fresh downloads, right on the device.

    October 20, 2026. GET MORE brings packs, sounds and skins straight to your MIKO.

    New

    • GET MORE: genre packs, patterns, sounds, templates, skins, soundfonts and demo projects, downloaded on the device, with UPDATE and REMOVE. GET MORE
    • A mixer strip per instrument, with a fader, PAN, ❚❚, S and M. The mixer
    • SETTINGS, tidied into GENERAL, KEYS, LOOK, LIVE FX, LIVE REC, PROFILE, CONNECTIONS and HELP & ABOUT. Settings
    • New skins, PASTEL NIGHT the default, each a plain file you can share or make. Skins, Make a skin set
    • The device's keys run the transport; TRANSPORT ON SCREEN brings the buttons back.
    • SINGING or VOICE-OVER for the LIVE REC mic, each with its own effects. Talking over the music
    • ALSO AS TEMPLATE when you save, and an AUTHOR on every export. Templates

    ravving 0.4

    Thirty pads to play with!

    October 14, 2026. LIVE FX grows to thirty pads, SETTINGS gets its own page, and ravving speaks Spanish and Japanese.

    New

    • Thirty LIVE FX pads in two banks, from KILL LOW to FREEZE, on ALT and the step keys. LIVE FX, Play it live
    • SETTINGS as a page, and FIXED KNOBS: PICK UP, JUMP or SCALE. Fixed knobs
    • A new look drawn by ravving itself, with each page in its own colours.
    • Spanish and Japanese, the whole app and this webpage. Languages
    • LOOP or ONCE for a pattern shorter than its section. Loop or once
    • More ways to send: any number of computers, a phone by QR code, and your library on a phone. Browse from your phone
    • New packs: Body, Hollywood, R&B, Droon, Bebop and Solos. The genre packs

    Fixed

    • A Bluetooth output settles before the sound goes to it, and a silent output is reported in the issues center. Audio output
    • A number key on the SONG page, the mixer, the player or the RECORDER goes back to the page you had open.
    • The fixed knobs pick a value up in the control center too, so nothing jumps.
    • A pad's notes stop before its sample changes.
    • The metronome no longer clicks after the effects.
    • WITH ITS SOUND and WITH ITS MODS dim when a preset keeps none. Loading a preset

    ravving 0.3

    Lights, camera, groove!

    October 11, 2026. Film your sessions and send files anywhere.

    New

    • Video recording of the screen or a USB camera, with the sound in sync. Film your session
    • SEND… to a USB stick, your computer, or a phone by QR code. Send a file to your phone
    • FILES: your whole library by kind, to send or delete. Files
    • A new project starts clean on every page. A new project starts clean
    • The Vuelo pack, nu-disco with a template for every key. The genre packs
