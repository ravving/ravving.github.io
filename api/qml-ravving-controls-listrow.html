    Detailed Description

    A faint card at the surfaces' corners, lighter while pressed or open, tinted in the accent while current (the track playing, the recording picked), ringed while the device's keys are on it (highlighted). What it shows is its children (they sit over its tap); a tap anywhere else is clicked. FileRow (FILES, USB, GET MORE) is one, and so are the rows of Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, ISSUES, the RECORDER's recordings, the music player's lists and the drop-down lists' entries.

    Property Documentation

    accent : color

    The current one's tint and, unless ringColor says otherwise, the ring's; the light the card is made of.

    baseColor : color [read-only]

    The card's colour as the row's state makes it (a row tinting itself further, as a FileRow's folder, starts from it).

    current : bool

    The device's keys are on it (the ring); it is the one in use (the tint); it shows more under it (lighter).

    doubleTappable : bool

    A hold is its own gesture (pressAndHold; off, a long tap is still a tap), and a second quick tap is one too (doubleClicked; off, it is another tap). Off unless the list wants them.

    flat : bool

    No card at rest (a drop-down's entries, apart by hairlines): only the press, the tint and the ring show.

    held : bool

    Held down by its owner's own tap (a delegate's background: interactive off).

    highlighted : bool

    The device's keys are on it (the ring); it is the one in use (the tint); it shows more under it (lighter).

    holdable : bool

    A hold is its own gesture (pressAndHold; off, a long tap is still a tap), and a second quick tap is one too (doubleClicked; off, it is another tap). Off unless the list wants them.

    ink : color

    The current one's tint and, unless ringColor says otherwise, the ring's; the light the card is made of.

    interactive : bool

    Whether it takes a tap itself (off: a delegate's background, the delegate taps), and whether it is held down.

    open : bool

    The device's keys are on it (the ring); it is the one in use (the tint); it shows more under it (lighter).

    pressed : bool [read-only]

    Whether it takes a tap itself (off: a delegate's background, the delegate taps), and whether it is held down.

    ringColor : color

    The current one's tint and, unless ringColor says otherwise, the ring's; the light the card is made of.
