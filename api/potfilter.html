    Detailed Description

    A pot left alone still reads a few raw steps up and down. Reported, each wiggle would set whatever the knob drives on the page in focus to the pot's position (a drum row's level at the pot's 2 %), so a resting pot reports nothing until it moves more than RestDeadBand raw steps from where it was last reported (or, before its first report, from where it rested once the history filled). Once turning, until RestMs pass without a report, a reading more than JitterAmount steps from the recent average counts, so a turn keeps its fine steps. The last EndZone steps at either end snap to the end, and reaching an end while turning always counts, so a control's minimum and maximum are always reached. Nothing is reported until HistoryLength readings came in.

    Plain logic with times in milliseconds from any clock that only goes forward (HardwareKnobs feeds it).
