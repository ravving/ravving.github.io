    Detailed Description

    BeatRepeat runs it in its JACK callback, after the repeat, so a loop held under REVERSE plays backwards. The GUI thread switches effects with setEffects() and gives the transport's grid with setGrid(); the audio thread reads only atomics and buffers allocated by prepare(), takes no lock and allocates nothing.

    The ring and the head

    Every frame of the input goes into a stereo ring (twice the longest beat, LongestBeatSeconds: 2.7 s at 48 kHz). One head at a time reads it instead of the live input:

    • REVERSE: each beat plays the beat before it backwards, on the grid's beats;
    • REWIND: the tape slows, stops and runs back faster and faster (three times the speed after 0.35 s) until the ring's past runs out, then silence until the pad lets go;
    • TAPE STOP: the tape slows to a stop over a beat, its level with its speed, then silence;
    • HALF: half speed, an octave down; every two beats it starts again from the first;
    • OCTAVE UP: an octave up, two 40 ms grains crossing;
    • WOBBLE: the tape's speed wavers four times a second, 4 ms either way around 16 ms.

    The rest, in stereo, in this order

    FLANGER (a 0.7 to 4.7 ms delay with feedback swept once a bar), ECHO (a dotted 8th with darkening feedback; what is in it rings out after the pad lets go), FREEZE (a small feedback-delay reverb that takes in one 16th of the mix, then holds it; let go, it fades in about a second), the KILL LOW / MID / HIGH isolator (Linkwitz–Riley crossovers at 250 Hz and 2.44 kHz), PHONE (500 Hz to 2.5 kHz, mono, driven), ALIAS (held to 6 kHz with no filtering), GATE (open for the first half of every 16th), AUTO-PAN (the mix in mono, across and back once a beat), MONO and FADE OUT (to silence over two beats).

    Every switch, and every jump of the head, is crossfaded over FadeFrames. A filter stage runs 256 frames from rest before it fades in, so its start never sounds. With nothing on and no tail ringing, process() only fills the ring and passes the input through.

    bool LiveFxEngine::isIdle() const

    Returns whether nothing is on and nothing rings out: process() then only fills the ring. The audio thread's state (tests read it between cycles).

    void LiveFxEngine::prepare(double sampleRate)

    Allocates the buffers and sets the filters for sampleRate. Call it before the audio thread runs process(), never while it does.

    void LiveFxEngine::process(const float *inLeft, const float *inRight, float *outLeft, float *outRight, quint32 frames, quint64 startFrame)

    Runs one cycle of frames frames starting at the audio clock's startFrame: inLeft and inRight in, outLeft and outRight out (they may be the same buffers). Audio thread.

    double LiveFxEngine::sampleRate() const

    Returns the rate prepare() was given.

    void LiveFxEngine::setEffects(quint32 mask)

    Switches the effects to mask, a bit() per effect on; any thread. Of the head effects (isHead()) only the first one set plays.

    See also effects().

    void LiveFxEngine::setGrid(quint64 barStart, double framesPerStep)

    Gives the transport's grid: barStart, a frame a bar started at (or the moment of a press, the transport stopped), and framesPerStep, a 16th's length. Any thread; the audio thread takes the pair whole at its next cycle.
