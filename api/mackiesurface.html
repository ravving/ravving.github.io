    Detailed Description

    Its eight channel strips are the instruments, in the order ControllerMixers has them. On each strip:

    • the fader is the instrument's level, as the surface labels it. A fader has no motor: it takes the level over once it reaches it (within PickupWindow, or crossing it), so a fader left elsewhere does not jump the level; a level changed anywhere else lets go of it until it is reached again. With the pick-up off (setFaderPickup(), the controller's pickup setting) any move sets the level. The level's position goes back to the fader, so the surface's own light above it shows whether the two agree. Near 0 dB (UnityZone) is 0 dB.
    • the knob, an endless V-Pot, is the instrument's pan: each tick PanStep left or right, faster as the knob turns faster (RelativeEncoder), from where the pan is. Turned through the middle it stops there once; pressed (a surface whose knobs press) it goes back to the middle.
    • the buttons are the instrument's mute (M), solo (S), take (R: ● on the instrument's strip, flashing while it counts in) and stop (the fourth: its pattern paused, ❚❚). Each acts on its press.

    Every light follows the instruments whatever changes them, and the transport's PLAY (lit while playing), STOP (lit while stopped) and RECORD (a take or LIVE REC: flashing while it counts in, lit while it records). △ and ▽ show the previous and the next machine, ◀◀ and ▶▶ skip a bar back and on. The transport keys themselves, ◁ ▷ and the bank keys are left to MidiInput's learned keys (the surface's defaults in data/midi-devices.json: PLAY plays and pauses, STOP stops).

    What goes out is what changed only: each light and fader is sent when its state differs from what it was last sent, and changes waiting to go are merged (a fader moving fast sends its latest position). They go in small batches (setPacing()), a resync on connecting included, which a Bluetooth link takes in its stride. Nothing runs while nothing changes. GUI thread only.

    [override virtual] bool MackieSurface::receiveMidi(int size, quint8 status, quint8 data1, quint8 data2)

    Reimplements: ControllerDriver::receiveMidi(int size, quint8 status, quint8 data1, quint8 data2).

    Takes the surface's message of size bytes status data1 data2 if it is a channel strip's fader, knob or button, or a key of its own (△ ▽, ◀◀ ▶▶), and returns whether it did. Anything else (the transport keys, ◁ ▷) is left to MidiInput.

    void MackieSurface::setClock(const std::function<qint64 ()> &now)

    Has the knobs' turns timed by now (milliseconds, monotonic), a clock of the caller's: for tests. Unset, the surface times them itself.

    void MackieSurface::setFaderPickup(bool pickup)

    Has every fader wait to reach its level before it takes it (pickup on, the default), or set the level on any move (off): FaderPickup::setPickup().

    See also faderPickup().

    void MackieSurface::setPacing(int gapMs, int perFlush, int faderGapMs = 30)

    Sends at most perFlush messages at a time, gapMs apart, and a fader's position at most once every faderGapMs (8 ms, 12 and 30 ms by default: a resync of every light and fader in a few batches, a moving level at about 30 a second).

    [static] DriverSpec MackieSurface::spec()

    Returns how the driver is added to ControllerDrivers: for the controllers whose settings name mackie as their surface, remade when their knobs' encoding or their faders' pick-up changes.

    [override virtual] void MackieSurface::start()

    Reimplements: ControllerDriver::start().

    Starts hosting: the surface needs no handshake, so it is ready at once. What it shows goes out as it comes (followStrip(), followTransport()); a strip with no instrument is dark.

    [override virtual] void MackieSurface::stop()

    Reimplements: ControllerDriver::stop().

    Stops hosting: the surface's lights go out, and what was waiting is dropped. What it knew of the surface goes too (where its faders are, which held which level, the knobs' speed): when it connects again, every light and fader is sent again and each fader picks its level up anew.
