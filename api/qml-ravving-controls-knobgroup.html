    Detailed Description

    It is a FocusScope: while it has active focus (arrow keys or a tap), its frame lights, each target shows a KnobBadge, and its Hardware handlers receive the knobs: - small knob n drives targets[n]; - the big knob drives targets[3], or fine-tunes targets[0] when there are only three; a group with ownsBigKnob false leaves it to the item above (a page's volume).

    A target is any item with value, from, to, knobStep (per detent), fineStep (per big-knob detent), knobBadge (-1 or the knob shown) and knobPending — Knob and LedDisplay provide them.

    Fixed pots (Z2_V5B) use pick-up: after the group gains focus, a pot changes nothing until it reaches or crosses the target's value, so values never jump.

    A group can instead (or also) hold navItems, buttons a knob steps through (navKnob, the big knob unless set; a fixed pot picks the button at its position): the highlighted one wears that knob's badge and SELECT activates it. A nav item needs navHighlighted, navKnob, navPosition (its place in the list, 0..1, drawn as the badge arc) and activate() — Chip and InstrumentChip provide them.

    Property Documentation

    accent : color

    The group has the focus, and the knobs may reach it without it (borrowed: ALT does that to the header's FX SENDS); either way it shows its badges and frame. accent colours that frame and its badges (with none given, the skin's accent, when there is one).

    borrowed : bool

    The group has the focus, and the knobs may reach it without it (borrowed: ALT does that to the header's FX SENDS); either way it shows its badges and frame. accent colours that frame and its badges (with none given, the skin's accent, when there is one).

    chromeHeight : real [read-only]

    What the group adds around its controls (the frame's padding, the title over them), measured from the items, so an owner can give the controls exactly the rest.

    chromePadding : real [read-only]

    What the group adds around its controls (the frame's padding, the title over them), measured from the items, so an owner can give the controls exactly the rest.

    chromeWidth : real [read-only]

    What the group adds around its controls (the frame's padding, the title over them), measured from the items, so an owner can give the controls exactly the rest.

    content : alias [default]

    The controls; laid out in a row under the title.

    controlSpacing : real [read-only]

    The gap between the controls in the row.

    focused : bool [read-only]

    The group has the focus, and the knobs may reach it without it (borrowed: ALT does that to the header's FX SENDS); either way it shows its badges and frame. accent colours that frame and its badges (with none given, the skin's accent, when there is one).

    framed : bool

    Whether the group lights its own frame while the knobs are here: off where what holds it shows that already (a MIXER strip's border); the badges show either way.

    knobsHere : bool [read-only]

    The group has the focus, and the knobs may reach it without it (borrowed: ALT does that to the header's FX SENDS); either way it shows its badges and frame. accent colours that frame and its badges (with none given, the skin's accent, when there is one).

    navIndex : int

    Items the big knob steps through, and the highlighted one.

    navItems : var

    Items the big knob steps through, and the highlighted one.

    navKnob : int

    The knob that steps through navItems: 0–2 a small knob, 3 the big one.

    ownsBigKnob : bool

    Whether the big knob works on this group, or passes on (unaccepted) to the item above.

    pickUp : PickUp [read-only]

    The fixed pots' pick-up (PickUp, shared with the control center): a pot changes nothing until it comes to the value or crosses it.

    targets : var

    The controls the knobs drive, in knob order.

    title : string

    Shown above the controls.

    Method Documentation

    move(knob, position)

    Device knob knob, a fixed pot, moved to position (0..1).

    refreshBadges()

    Puts each target's KnobBadge (and each nav item's highlight) where the group's knobs are now; the group calls it itself when that changes.

    targetFor(knob)

    Returns the target device knob knob drives (0–2: knobs 1–3, 3: the big knob), or null.

    turn(knob, delta)

    Device knob knob, an endless one, turned by delta detents; returns false when the group has no use for it.
