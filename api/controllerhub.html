    ControllerHub Class

    The app side of one controller capability (ControllerOctaves, ControllerScales): the one object the app talks to for it. ControllerDrivers offers each hub every driver while it runs (attach(), detach()); the hub takes the drivers with its capability. A capability no controller has leaves its hub empty, and what the app tells it goes nowhere. More...
