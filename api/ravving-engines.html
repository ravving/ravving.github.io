    Engines and effects

    AudioInsert

    Something audio goes through on the JACK graph, with stereo or mono inputs and outputs

    BeatRepeat

    A beat repeat on the master for LiveFx's STUTTER and LOOP pads: ravving's own JACK client (ravving-repeat, stereo in and out), the first of the master inserts

    ConsoleProcess

    A helper process ravving drives through its text console (FluidSynth's shell, jalv's command line), run by libzynthbox's ProcessWrapper

    EffectChain

    Effects chain every machine has, in signal order: DRIVE, COMPRESSOR, REVERB, EQ, PAN, SIDECHAIN, DELAY, each with its knobs

    EffectChain::Effect

    An effect of the chain: its name and its knobs, in order

    EffectChain::Knob

    A knob of an effect: its name, its defaultValue (0..1) and whether it is shared by every machine

    FluidSynthEngine

    A FluidSynth process playing one soundfont preset (SF2 or SF3), such as a drum machine's kit or a soundfont sound source

    GlobalEffects

    Studio's two send effects: one reverb and one delay that every machine sends into, their returns mixed into the master

    JalvEngine

    An LV2 instrument as a machine's sound engine, run by jalv (a JalvPlugin)

    JalvPlugin

    One LV2 plugin running in its own jalv process on the JACK graph: an effect (a reverb, a delay..., audio in and out) or an instrument (MIDI in, audio out; JalvEngine)

    LiveFx

    Performance effects on the whole mix, switched by pads, and three send levels; published to QML as studio.liveFx

    SoundEngine

    A sound engine a machine plays through: a process with a MIDI input and a stereo output on the JACK graph

    SoundFontFile

    What an SF2 or SF3 soundfont says about its presets, read without loading its samples

    SoundFontFile::Preset

    One preset of a soundfont, as FluidSynth selects it: its name, bank and program

    SoundPresets

    An LV2 plugin's factory presets, read from its bundle's presets file

    SoundPresets::Preset

    One factory preset: its name (without its kind's prefix), title (without its author either), category (what the prefix names: PADS, KEYS...) and controls (the port values, symbol to value)

    TrackEffects

    One machine's effects (the EffectChain), on its track's JackPassthrough, the processing every libzynthbox track has; published to QML as machine.effects
