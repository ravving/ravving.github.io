    Detailed Description

    While armed, takesKey() consumes the RECORD and STOP keys: RECORD starts a recording, STOP ends it (and the music), and a second STOP within three seconds (stopPressed(), SecondPress) leaves LIVE REC: armed off, the mic's line given back. How a recording is made:

    • The sound is tapped from the master's last outputs (after its inserts) by AudioTap, ravving's own JACK client with a ring of 2^20 frames (about 21 s at 48 kHz), and fed to ffmpeg through a named pipe as raw float samples. It does not go through AudioLevels, which stays free for other recorders, nor through ffmpeg's JACK input, whose small ring a storage stall would overrun.
    • The picture is ffmpeg's x11grab of the display (or v4l2 of the camera), encoded with libx264 on two threads under nice. qualities lists the tiers.
    • Sync: ffmpeg stamps every frame with the wall clock (-use_wallclock_as_timestamps), the tap records the wall-clock time of its first sample (AudioTap::startRealtimeUs()), which ffmpeg gets as -itsoffset; with -copyts both streams share one clock in the file. The recording is written to a Matroska file first (readable after a crash), then a -c copy remux cuts the head up to the first video frame and writes the MP4 with its index at the front (+faststart) and its tags.

    The states (state): off (not armed), ready (armed), countdown, starting (ffmpeg launching), recording and saving (the remux). finished() or failed() reports the end.

    The settings, stored under ravving/video, are mode, source, quality, countdown, camera, mic (the live recording's own input path, input, a LiveRecMic: while LIVE REC is on with MIC it holds the device input for itself and feeds the tap's mic ports through its own effects; nothing of the RECORDER's reaches the recording), the voice-over levels and showKeys. settingsChanged() is emitted again on a language change, so translated labels are re-read. GUI thread only.

    [invokable] void VideoRecorder::stop()

    Ends the recording, which is saved, and stops the transport; or cancels the countdown, saving nothing. The recorder stays armed.

    Note: This function can be invoked via the meta-object system and from QML. See Q_INVOKABLE.

    [invokable] bool VideoRecorder::stopPressed()

    STOP, pressed while armed (the device's key through takesKey(), or the transport's stop): a recording or its countdown under way is stopped (stop(): the music stops with it) and the press is consumed; a second press within three seconds (SecondPress) leaves LIVE REC (setArmed(false): the mic's line goes back) and is consumed too. A lone STOP with nothing recording arms that second press and is not consumed, so the transport stops as it would. Returns whether the press was consumed; false when not armed.

    Note: This function can be invoked via the meta-object system and from QML. See Q_INVOKABLE.

    bool VideoRecorder::takesKey(const QString &cuia)

    Offers the device key cuia to the recorder before anything else handles it. Returns true when it was consumed: while armed, SWITCH_RECORD_RELEASED (starts a recording when idle) and SWITCH_STOP_RELEASED while a recording or its countdown runs (stopPressed() ends it). A STOP with nothing recording is not consumed: it reaches the transport's stop, which asks stopPressed() itself, so the press counts once.

    [invokable] void VideoRecorder::toggle()

    Toggles the recorder: when not armed, starts a recording (start()); during a recording, stops it and disarms; when armed and idle, disarms.

    Note: This function can be invoked via the meta-object system and from QML. See Q_INVOKABLE.
