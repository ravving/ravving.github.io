    AlbumArt::Layout Struct

    struct AlbumArt::Layout

    Where the parts of a cover land, as layout() works them out. More...

    Header: #include <albumart.h>
    • Layout is part of Audio, recording and export.

    Detailed Description

    FieldMeaning
    sizeThe cover's width and height, in pixels.
    artworkWhich artwork, in artworks(); -1 when there is none.
    variantThe look: the sidebar or the corner.
    sidebarThe sidebar's column, down the left edge; empty in the corner look.
    partsThe sidebar's parts, top to bottom (Part); none in the corner look.
    boxStickerThe sticker in the sidebar's box when there is no icon, in stickers(); -1 for none.
    titleThe title as drawn ("Untitled" for an empty one).
    titleFontThe display family at the size the title fits at (the corner look's).
    titleRectThe box the title's lines take, at the cover's foot (the corner look's; empty in the sidebar's).
    labelBlockThe label block's plate, in the bottom-right corner (the corner look's).
    iconRectThe project's icon, in the sidebar's box or the plate; empty without an icon.
    markRectThe RAVV wordmark in the plate.
    creditsRectThe framed box under the logos; empty without credits.
    creditsIts lines as drawn, upper case and cut to its width: the tempo and the key, then the sounds (either left out when unknown).
    creditFontsEach credits line's face: the mono for the tempo and key, the text face for the sounds.
    spineThe spine's text, up the left edge: the author, else the title.
    spineFontThe spine's face: the text face, semibold and spaced.
    spineRectThe box the spine takes, upright.
    stickersThe stickers, in the order they are stuck on.
