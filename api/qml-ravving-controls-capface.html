    CapFace QML Type

    The face of a key on a grid: a raised cap in a skin, with an LED strip along its top. More...

    Detailed Description

    What KeyCap draws as its background, shared with every key that sits on a grid as the step keys do (the SONG page's section keys and song blocks), so they all look alike. The cap sinks onto its shadow while down; lit lights its LED strip in ledColor (with a bloom), and playhead washes it white. Its corners are the controls' (Theme.radius).

    The LED's colour, and how much of it shows while the LED is off (0: the plain dark strip; a section key keeps its colour dimly so it is told apart).

    skin : var
