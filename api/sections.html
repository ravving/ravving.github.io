    Detailed Description

    Sections are live: the section on screen is the one the machines play and edit. Picking another keeps what was edited in the one left (captured from the machines) and hands the machines the one picked. What a section holds of a machine is Machine::sectionContent(): the pattern and whether the machine rests, never its sound.

    Sections knows no machine: it goes through a Host (the studio) that captures, applies and measures the machines' content, so it is tested on its own (tests/tst_sections.cpp).

    The song is a list of entries, each a section and how many times it repeats; a section may appear in it any number of times, and a new section joins its end. A section's length is its longest sounding pattern unless it is given one (1 to 8 bars). The project keeps it all (state(), restore()).

    In SONG mode the transport plays the song: before it starts, the first entry's section is put in place (songStarting()); then bar by bar (barStarted(), the transport's absolute bar), as an entry's last bar begins the next entry's section is cued for the bar after (the studio's bar-line switch, a whole bar ahead), and as the song's last bar begins the studio is told to stop at its end. A section picked by hand while the song plays jumps there (to its first entry; with none, back to LOOP). playFrom() starts the song at any entry.

    When a pick lands while playing (switchAt, kept with the project): on the next bar (SwitchBar, the default), or when the section playing finishes its pass (SwitchSection: the pick waits in queue and is cued as that pass's last bar begins, as the song cues its entries). In LOOP the picks queue up, up to MaxQueue in the order picked, each played for one pass; in SONG one waits, a new pick taking its place. Stopped, a pick is immediate.

    
 studio.sections.add()                 // a new, empty section after the one on screen
 studio.sections.duplicate(id)         // a copy of a section, made current
 studio.sections.pick(id)              // play and edit another section
 studio.sections.setRepeats(entry, 2)  // a song entry played twice

    As a HistoryParticipant ("SECTIONS" in studio.history) it undoes the arrangement, not what plays: the sections (id, name, colour, length) and the song, but not the section on screen (a pick is no step: undo never switches what plays) nor the machines' content (their own history has it). A removed section keeps its content, so undoing the removal brings it back whole. It is not a QML type (the backend is outside the QML module); QML reads the mode as Rave.sectionsLoop and Rave.sectionsSong. GUI thread only.
