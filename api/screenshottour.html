    ScreenshotTour Class

    The screenshot tour: a development aid that drives the interface through a list of steps, saves a PNG of the window after each, and quits. More...

    Detailed Description

    main.cpp starts it when RAVVING_SCREENSHOT_DIR names a folder; it works offscreen too. A step is an action and, optionally, the name of the shot taken once it has settled (700 ms; 2.5 s after switching pages, which load lazily). The steps come in two parts:

    • the scripted steps: the Ravving pages and machines through Ravving's setters, knob turns and key presses through simulateKnob() and simulateCuia(), and QML properties of items found by object name;
    • the popup pass, after switching to each page and to each full-page view that Session's view flags open: every QQuickPopup in the scene, and inside closed popups, that has not been visited yet is opened, shot and closed. Its shot is named d-<view>-<type>-<id> after its QML type and id, so new popups need no step of their own.

    A shot waits up to 6 s while a transient notice item is visible in the scene, since such items come and go on their own timers. index.txt in the folder lists the shots in order. When RAVVING_STYLE_DUMP names a QML file, its dump() result (a JSON string) is written to style-dump.json before the first step.
