    Detailed Description

    hold() sends the look's waiting frame once (nothing moves while loading, so the loading keeps the whole machine); play() runs the show's frames, about 30 a second, timed from the moment the screen's show starts, and finished() says it is over. Frames go out through frameReady(); StartupSplash runs the show on a thread of its own and hands them straight to Leds::showFrame(), so the busy GUI thread around the show's start does not hold the lights up.

    The shows are meant to be lively: a white flash on each beat (a cut, a landing, a step), a chase, a march or sparkles while they move, and a sweep from the row's ends into its middle that turns the keys off at the end.

    Every frame is a function of the time alone (frameAt()), so a late tick drops frames and never stretches the show. Only the keys whose place is sure on every kit are used: the step keys, a row from 1 on the left to 16 on the right (sweeps and halves; the Z2_V4 kit lights steps 1 to 5 only, and the row is those five), the number keys 1 to 5, and the rest together.

    The timelines follow SplashLook.qml's animations step for step; a change to a show's durations there changes them here.

    Member Function Documentation

    SplashLights::SplashLights(const QString &style, int pick, int steps, QObject *parent = nullptr)

    Makes the lights for the splash look style (StartupSplash::styles()) with its pick (the logo, or ARU's character) on a kit whose first steps step keys have lights (16, or 5 on the Z2_V4 kit), with the given parent. Nothing is lit until hold() or play().

    [static] int SplashLights::aruChoices()

    Returns how many ARU picks there are: each species (the octopus, the wolf pup, the alien) in each look (the plain hello and the party's seven moods).

    [static] QStringList SplashLights::aruFrames(int pick)

    Returns ARU's frames for pick (0 to aruChoices() - 1): the one it waits on, then the show's eight steps, each the stem of a PNG in data/branding/splash (aru-wolf-tapL, aru-party-alien-techno-rave1). The species is pick modulo 3, the look the rest: 0 the plain hello, then hiphop, gregorian, pop, punk, techno, jazz and metal, the mood's loop repeated over the eight steps.

    [static] QList<int> SplashLights::aruHolds(int pick)

    Returns how long each of ARU's eight steps holds for pick, in ms: the plain hello's own rhythm, or the mood's pace (at most 300 ms a frame).

    [static] QVariantList SplashLights::aruParty(int pick)

    Returns the party on the stage while the studio loads for pick: three dancers, the pick's species in its look in the middle (so the show can take over from it), the other two species beside it in moods of their own, each a map of species, frames (PartySteps PNG stems, its loop repeated) and pace (the beats a frame lasts, the About page's). The stage (SplashStage.qml) shows each dancer in another of its frames at every startup step.

    [static] int SplashLights::duration(const QString &style, int pick = 0)

    Returns how long style's show runs (with its pick: ARU's look), in ms, as SplashLook.qml's does; 0 for off or an unknown style.

    [signal] void SplashLights::finished()

    The show is over: the keys go back to what the page lights.

    [static] SplashLights::Frame SplashLights::frameAt(const QString &style, int pick, int ms, int steps = 16)

    Returns the frame of style's show (with its pick) at ms after it started, on a kit with steps lit step keys (1–16); a negative ms is the frame while the studio loads, and ms at or past duration() all keys off.

    [signal] void SplashLights::frameReady(const SplashLights::Frame &frame)

    The keys should show frame now.

    void SplashLights::hold()

    Sends the frame shown while the studio loads, once.

    void SplashLights::play()

    Starts the show from its first frame; a second call does nothing.
