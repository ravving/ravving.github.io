    Audio, recording and export

    AlbumArt

    Cover art for a track: one of ravving's artworks, the title, its real credits and at most one sticker

    AlbumArt::Credits

    What a track's cover says about it: who made it and with what. The exporter fills it from the session (Exporter::coverCredits())

    AlbumArt::Layout

    Where the parts of a cover land, as layout() works them out

    AlbumArt::Part

    One part of a cover's sidebar, as layout() places it

    AlbumArt::Sticker

    One sticker on a cover, as layout() places it

    AlbumArtProvider

    Serves AlbumArt to QML as image://albumart/<title>%09<icon>%09<seed>%09<author>%09<bpm>%09<key>%09<sounds>%09<date>%09<style>

    AudioFile

    Audio-file operations on libsndfile (WAV, FLAC, Ogg, AIFF...) for the samplers and the exporter

    AudioFile::Cut

    Result of writeCut(): an export cut from a raw recording

    AudioFile::Info

    A file's length in frames, sample rate and channel count, as info() reads them

    AudioFile::Take

    Result of processTake(): whether it worked, and the take's last burst of sound as a slot trim

    AudioGraph

    Audio graph's clock: the sample rate and period PipeWire's JACK graph runs at

    AudioOutputs

    Where the sound goes: the device's jacks, an HDMI screen, or a connected Bluetooth speaker or headphones; published to QML as outputs

    AudioTap

    A JACK client that copies the master's output into a ring buffer and feeds it to ffmpeg through a named pipe; VideoRecorder's audio source

    Connection

    One way a controller is plugged in: a port the router lists, with its name as the router has it, its key (ConnectionRegistry::keyOf()), its Transport and the port what is sent through it goes to

    ConnectionMultiplexer

    Every controller's messages as one stream (midi(), sysex()), each stamped with its controller and connection, a message come twice over a controller's several connections passed on once: the one reader of what the router says controllers send

    ConnectionRegistry

    Controllers plugged in (studio.controllers), each one Controller however many connections it has, read from the router's list of ports: the one reader of what the router says about controllers coming and going, and the one place a port's name is understood

    Controller

    A controller as ravving knows it: one ControllerId however many connections it has at once (a cable's several ports, its radio beside its cable), its profile's label when it has one, and the primary connection, the one what is sent to it goes through

    ControllerCapability

    What every capability interface of a controller has: which controller it is (controller()). A ControllerDriver implements the interfaces of what its controller can do (ControllerOctave, ControllerScale), and overrides controller() once for all of them

    ControllerDriver

    A controller ravving knows how to talk to: the base of each driver (RoliBlocks), with the System Exclusive transport, request / reply pairing, a keep-alive and the 7-bit packing the drivers share

    ControllerDrivers

    Controllers ravving has a driver for (studio.drivers): one ControllerDriver per controller a driver fits, started when the controller is plugged in, stopped when it goes or ravving quits

    ControllerHub

    App side of one controller capability (ControllerOctaves, ControllerScales): the one object the app talks to for it. ControllerDrivers offers each hub every driver while it runs (attach(), detach()); the hub takes the drivers with its capability. A capability no controller has leaves its hub empty, and what the app tells it goes nowhere

    ControllerId

    What names a controller, whichever way it is plugged in: a known controller's label (ControllerProfiles), the same for its cable and its radio, else the normalised name of its one connection (ConnectionRegistry::keyOf())

    ControllerMixer

    A controller with mixer strips, a control surface: each strip follows an instrument (followStrip()), the transport buttons the transport (followTransport()), and the strips' controls come back as requests (askStrip(), askTransport()). Its driver implements it (MackieSurface)

    ControllerMixers

    Controllers with mixer strips (studio.mixers): the instruments' mix goes to each of them, and their strips' controls come back as requests

    ControllerOctave

    A controller with an octave of its own, as ControllerOctaves sees it: ravving's octave goes to it (followOctave()) and its own changes come back (requestOctave()), both only while linked (setOctaveLinked()). Whatever talks to the controller implements it (RoliBlocks)

    ControllerOctaves

    Controllers with an octave of their own (studio.octaves), linked to the current machine's octave: setOctave() goes to each linked one, and their own changes come back as octaveRequested(). Each controller can be unlinked by name (setLinked(), kept in the ravving/midi settings beside MidiInput's); unlinked, its octave buttons shift its own notes only. ControllerDrivers attaches each driver while it runs (a ControllerHub); add() links any other ControllerOctave. GUI thread only

    ControllerProfiles

    Known controllers' defaults (data/midi-devices.json): which profile a controller is, by its name and, when the profile says, the USB id of the sound card it is on

    ControllerScale

    A controller that plays in a key, a note grid (a Lightpad): ravving's key goes to it (followKey()). Its driver implements it (RoliBlocks)

    ControllerScales

    Controllers that play in a key (studio.scales): the project's key goes to each (setKey()), and to each one that starts later. GUI thread only

    ControllerSettings

    One controller's settings, as the user set them over its profile's defaults: what its messages become (MidiInput), whether its driver hosts it (ControllerDrivers), whether its octave follows ravving's (ControllerOctaves)

    ControllerSettingsStore

    Controllers' settings (ControllerSettings), one per ControllerId: the one reader and writer of the ravving/midi settings file

    DriverSpec

    How a protocol's driver is added to ControllerDrivers: its id, which controllers it is for (matches), how its ControllerDriver is made (make) and which of a controller's settings remake the device (remakeOn). A driver's own spec() makes one (RoliBlocks::spec(), MackieSurface::spec()); named() and surface() cover the two ways a driver finds its controllers

    EncoderGuess

    A CC learned for a value that may be an endless knob's or a position's (a fader, an absolute knob): which one, told from what it sends (hear())

    Exporter

    Project's exports, published to QML as studio.exporter: a track (an audio file) or a bundle (a .ravv zip), both into the library's exports folder (Paths::exports()), where the music player finds them

    FaderPickup

    A fader (or an absolute knob) with no motor, on a value it does not show: it takes the value over once it reaches it (within a window, or crossing it), so one left elsewhere does not jump the value; a value changed anywhere else lets go of it until it is reached again. The value it asked for coming back is not a change (asked()). With the pick-up off (setPickup()) it takes the value on any move instead: the fader always wins

    HardwarePorts

    A sound card's ports on the JACK graph, found by the card, never by a fixed JACK name

    InputCard

    Device's input card as its two users share it: the input modes, each mode's gain range and default, and the card's mixer (amixer)

    InputEffects

    Effects on the device input: a chain of LV2 plugins (jalv) between the input and whoever hears it (the monitor and the take, or the live recording's tap), so what is heard is what is recorded

    InputMixer

    Input card's mixer set from a knob: the last setting asked for goes to the card (InputCard::apply()) once the knob has rested for 60 ms, so a fast turn starts one amixer, not one per detent

    InputRecorder

    Device's audio input, recorded into the samplers or kept as played (LiveRecordings); published to QML as studio.input

    JackWiring

    Keeps the JACK graph wired for ravving's tracks, with the topology libravving's tracks expect (the one zynthbox-qml's autoconnect produces for a synth on a sketchpad track)

    KeyWatch

    Warns when a controller plays in another key than the project's (studio.keyWatch)

    LearnedMessage

    A controller's message as learned for a control: a note, a CC or a pitch bend on a channel, read from the bytes that come (of()) and written as the settings keep it (text(): "note 1 60", "cc 2 74", "bend 3"; parse())

    LearnedMixer

    A plain MIDI controller as the instruments' mixer: its keys, knobs and faders learned as the MIXER controls in SETTINGS · MIDI (MixerControl), linked to the instruments through ControllerMixers like a control surface's strips (MackieSurface)

    LearnedMixers

    Plain controllers with MIXER controls learned (SETTINGS · MIDI): a LearnedMixer for each one plugged in, linked to the instruments' mix (ControllerMixers, studio.mixers) while it has any, its lights sent through the MidiTransport

    LiveRecMic

    Live recording's own input path, published to QML as video.input: the mic (or guitar, or line) as the recording hears it, with its own mode, gain, role and effects, apart from the RECORDER's (InputRecorder)

    LiveRecordings

    Input takes kept as played (InputRecorder::record(true)), published to QML as studio.input.recordings

    MackieControl

    Mackie Control's messages, as a control surface in its DAW mode speaks them (a Donner STARRYCTRL): what comes in (decode()) and what goes back (light(), fader())

    MackieControl::Event

    A message from a Mackie Control surface, as decode() reads it: its kind, the channel strip it came from (strip), the fader's position or the V-Pot's CC (value), the first note of a button's eight (row) and whether it went down (pressed)

    MackieSurface

    A control surface in Mackie Control's DAW mode (a Donner STARRYCTRL) as the instruments' mixer: a ControllerDriver with mixer strips (ControllerMixer)

    Midi

    MIDI controllers' enumerations, shared by the backend and QML (the Midi type of the Ravving module: Midi.Ready, Midi.Hit…). Declared in midienums.h

    MidiEnums

    Registration of the Midi namespace's enumerations as the Ravving QML module's Midi type. Declared in qmltypes.h; nothing is declared in it

    MidiInput

    External MIDI controllers (keyboards, pads, grooveboxes) as a note input and as the device's keys and knobs, published to QML as studio.midiIn: what each message from a controller becomes, and each controller's settings

    MidiMessage

    A short MIDI message from a controller as the ConnectionMultiplexer hands it on: the controller it is from (ConnectionRegistry::idOf()), the connection it came on, its bytes and when it came

    MidiTransport

    Way to a controller: what a driver, a learned mixer or anything else sends to one goes through it, by the controller's id, with no knowledge of which port carries it

    MidiValues

    What a controller's bytes mean, the same wherever they are read (MidiInput, LearnedMixer, MackieSurface): where a CC presses a key (PressedFrom), a pitch bend as a fader's position (faderPosition()), the velocity and pressure curves a controller's settings pick (velocityCurve(), pressureCurve()), a bend scaled to the engines' range (scaledBend())

    MixerControl

    One of a mixer strip's controls a controller's key, knob or fader can be learned as (MidiInput's MIXER controls, LearnedMixer): a strip's volume, pan, mute, solo, take or pause, or the transport's ❚❚, a bar back or on, the previous or the next machine. Its id, as the settings keep it: MIXER1_VOLUME to MIXER8_PAUSE, MIXER_PAUSE, MIXER_BACK, MIXER_ON, MIXER_PREVIOUS, MIXER_NEXT

    MixerStrip

    One instrument as a controller's mixer strip sees it: level, pan, mute, solo, its pattern paused and its take

    MixerTransport

    Transport as a controller's transport buttons light it: playing or paused, and whether a take (or LIVE REC) counts in or records

    MusicLibrary

    Tracks on the device the music player plays from, published to QML as studio.music.library

    MusicPlayer

    Plays a queue of the library's tracks (MusicLibrary) one after the other through Qt Multimedia; published to QML as studio.music

    OutputWatch

    Checks that the sound sent to another output (AudioOutputs' loopback into a Bluetooth or HDMI sink) comes out of it

    PitchDetector

    Finds the pitch of a plucked string in a block of mono audio; Tuner's detector

    RelativeEncoder

    An endless knob on a MIDI controller: what its CC values say (decode()) and how far a tick goes once the knob turns fast (accelerate())

    RoliBlocks

    Ravving as the host of ROLI's BLOCKS (a Lightpad, with the control blocks on its DNA connectors): the handshake and keep-alive they need, the project's key and the current machine's octave set on their note grid, their octave buttons back, a Live Block's SCALE and octave buttons on the grid, and their buttons as the MIDI they sent before

    RouterTransport

    MidiTransport over libravving's router: what goes to a controller goes to the send target of its primary connection (ConnectionRegistry::sendTarget(): a Bluetooth device's ":in" port, a cable's own port), and nowhere while the controller is not plugged in

    SoundActivity

    Which tracks are sounding, or would be if they were not muted: what lights a machine's lamp (SoundLamp.qml) whatever its fader, mute, solo or pause

    TapTempo

    TAP tempo: the tempo from the gaps between taps (studio.tapTempo)

    Tuner

    Measures the pitch of a string played into the device input against a tuning's open strings; published to QML as studio.input.tuner

    VideoRecorder

    Records the screen or a USB camera with the master's sound into one MP4 in the videos folder (Paths::videos()), or the sound alone into a WAV; published to QML as video
