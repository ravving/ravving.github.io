    Detailed Description

    The current bank is the one that plays. Each loaded pad is one ClipAudioSource set up on its root slice from the pad's parameters; its key zone and root note are the pad's note (kits.json baseNote + pad, note 36 for pad 1 by default), so a pattern note or a live key plays exactly that pad. A sketchpad track has ten sample slots and its pattern reads only those, so pads 1 to 10 live on the machine's track and pads 11 to 16 on a second one (kits.json secondTrack). The machine keeps the second track's pattern in step with its own (length, loop, swing, the hits of pads 11 to 16) and sends those pads' live notes on that track. Both tracks' sample outputs are wired into the first track's lane, so the kit has one level, one pan and one effects chain (JackWiring::setSampleOutputs()).

    A pad's parameters are stored by tab and knob, each 0..1 (see tabs), and mapped to the slice: TRIM (start, end, loop point), MIX (volume as velocity, pan), AMP ENV (attack, decay or release), TUNE (semitones, fine), PLAY (mono or poly, mute group as an exclusivity group) and FILTER. AMP ENV's VEL SENS, TUNE's WARP, PLAY's OFFSET and the FLT ENV tab are stored but not applied. The pad flags (setFlag()): noteOn (plays while held) or one-shot, looping (from the loop point), reverse (plays a reversed copy of the file, rendered once into the cache folder), muted, soloed and sixteenLevels.

    FILTER puts one equaliser band (two for the 4-pole types) of the chosen type (OFF, CLASSIC, LPF2, LPF4, HPF2, HPF4, BPF2, BPF4) on the pad's clip at the cutoff, with the resonance as its Q; it takes effect as far as libzynthbox applies a clip's equaliser to its voices.

    audition() plays a pad once. setLatched() latches a pad: it switches to looping and its note is held until unlatched; stopAll() unlatches every pad. A latched pad ignores stopLive().

    The kit controls (kit, a PadKitControls): KIT volume (the machine's level), SEMI and FINE added to every pad's tune, and 16 LEVELS. With 16 LEVELS on (setSixteenLevels()), the sixteen keys all play the source pad, by type: VELOCITY (pad 1 softest, 16 hardest), TUNE (a semitone per pad, the original pitch on pad 4) or SCALE (steps of root and scale from pad 4). TUNE and SCALE use sixteen extra slices on the source pad's clip, each on its own note (LevelNoteBase + pad) with its pitch. Hits written while the mode is on are the bank's level hits, stored apart from the pads' own and exposed as steps meanwhile.

    The pad effects (heldFx, active while held) act on the clip of fxPad, so they affect every voice of that pad:

    • ECHO: the track's echo throw (TrackEffects::setEchoThrow()) at full while held;
    • TAPE STOP: the clip's speed sweeps down to 0.02 over 0.9 s, back to 1 on release;
    • SCRATCH: the speed sweeps between 0.45 and 1.6 while held;
    • STUTTER: the pad retriggers every 16th note of the tempo while held;
    • SKIP: the pad restarts an 8th note past its current position; its start is restored on release;
    • REVERSE: the pad plays its reversed copy from the mirrored position while held, and forwards again on release. The reversed copy must already exist; if it is still being rendered, nothing plays.

    The step pattern follows DrumMachine's interface (a row per pad, an accent row, per-pad level, mute and solo applied as velocities). Presets (pads-presets.json) are rhythms over the pads: keys "1" to "16" a row each, "x" a hit and "X" an accented one.

    PadSamplerMachine::PadSamplerMachine(const QVariantMap &config, const QVariantMap &state, PresetLibrary *presets, GlobalEffects *globalEffects, QObject *parent = nullptr)

    Constructs the pad sampler from config (its kits.json object, with baseNote and secondTrack) and the project's state, which also carries bank and banks (a list of maps with pads, steps, accents, bars and levels). presets, globalEffects and parent are as for Machine.

    [invokable] void PadSamplerMachine::applyChop(int pad, const QVariantList &boundaries, int firstPad = 0)

    Cuts the sample of pad at boundaries (sorted fractions of the file inside the pad's window; the window's ends are added) and puts the pieces on the pads from firstPad on, as many as fit: each piece is the same file with its own trim and the source pad's settings.

    Note: This function can be invoked via the meta-object system and from QML. See Q_INVOKABLE.
