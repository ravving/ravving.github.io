    Detailed Description

    A loopback whose sink is replaced (a codec switch) can keep every node running and every link in place while playing silence. Its JACK client (ravving-outwatch) listens to two ports while it watches: what is sent (the master, into the loopback) and what is played (the loopback's output). The process callback (audio thread) keeps each port's peak in an atomic; once a second the GUI thread takes them. silent() is emitted once music (a peak over -50 dBFS) has gone in for 6 s with nothing (under -90 dBFS) coming out, flowing() once sound comes out again. A silent master (stopped, a rest) proves nothing either way. While it is not watching its ports are disconnected.

    Member Function Documentation

    [explicit] OutputWatch::OutputWatch(QObject *parent = nullptr)

    Opens and activates the JACK client, with the given parent. Without JACK it logs a warning and never watches.

    [signal] void OutputWatch::flowing()

    Emitted when sound comes out again after silent().

    [signal] void OutputWatch::silent()

    Emitted once when music went in and nothing came out for 6 s.

    void OutputWatch::stop()

    Stops listening (the jacks are the output again, or the route is made again). Emits nothing.

    void OutputWatch::watch(const QString &sent, const QString &played)

    Starts listening to the JACK ports sent (what goes in) and played (what comes out), from now; a watch already running starts over.
