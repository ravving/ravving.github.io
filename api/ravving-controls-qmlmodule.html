    Ravving.Controls QML Types

    The base types ravving's pages and dialogs are built from: the buttons (ActionButton, Chip, ConfirmButton, CloseButton), the knob layer (Knob, Fader, LevelSlider, KnobGroup, PickUp), the readouts (Meter, KeyCap, Nameplate, Plate, LedDisplay, Toast, EmptyNote), the dialogs (Dialog, ChoiceDialog, ComboBox) and their parts (DialogHeader, SectionHeader, Caption). A plain Qt Quick Control with ravving's look comes from the style (Ravving.Style); these add what the style cannot.

    The kit knows nothing of the app. What it needs of the app's state comes in as properties, or through the Interaction singleton; it reads the design tokens and the skin from Ravving.Theme and the device's keys and knobs from Ravving.Input (the Hardware attached property).

    
 import Ravving.Controls

    ActionButton

    A button that DOES something once and leaves no state behind (LOAD, CLEAR, SAVE, RENAME, −/+, ◀ ▶, PLAY FROM HERE, TRIM, TO PADS…)

    ArmBar

    Countdown under a button that wants a second press

    BrowseList

    List of a browsing dialog (FILES, USB, GET MORE): FileRows in a column, a finger flicks it, a scroll bar when it needs one

    Caption

    Tiny spaced capitals that title each group of the top bar (TRANSPORT, TEMPO…)

    Chip

    A small pill toggle with an LED dot (FX chain, live FX pads, S/M, LIVE)

    ChoiceDialog

    A dialog to pick one item from a list, laid out as a grid of buttons (a drum kit, a preset, a sound, a project, a destination)

    CloseButton

    ✕ that leaves

    ComboBox

    A drop-down menu (ROOT, SCALE…) the device's keys can work

    ConfirmButton

    A button for what cannot be undone (DELETE, FORMAT, EJECT)

    DialFace

    A dial's face, drawn in vector so it stays crisp at any size (the theme's SVG dial blurs and breaks up at the 26–40 px the machines use)

    Dialog

    Base of ravving's dialogs (ChoicePopup, InputPopup)

    DialogHeader

    Head of a ravving dialog card

    EmptyNote

    What an empty list or view says, the same everywhere

    Fader

    A captioned horizontal fader

    FileRow

    A row of a browsing dialog's list (FILES, USB, GET MORE): a name over a line of detail, its actions at the right, the device's ring while the keys are on it

    FormRow

    One setting: its name and a line on what it does at the left, its control at the right

    Glyph

    A small line icon drawn in vector (no icon theme or emoji font needed on the device), for the control center and the mixer

    Interaction

    UI state the controls share, which the app reads and sets but the controls own

    KeyCap

    A drum-machine key

    KindList

    Kinds down a browsing dialog's left (FILES, USB, GET MORE): a row per kind with its name and how many it holds, the one shown on a tint with the accent's edge

    Knob

    A labelled knob

    KnobBadge

    Hardware-knob indicator

    KnobGroup

    A titled group of controls that the device knobs can take over

    LedDisplay

    A red seven-segment-style readout (the TEMPO display), with a caption under it (label, as a Knob has)

    LevelSlider

    A big pill slider in the manner of iOS's control center (brightness, the output level)

    Meter

    A peak meter in a dark channel

    Nameplate

    Machine's neon nameplate

    OnOffChip

    A Chip for one setting that is on or off: lit, and saying ON or OFF on itself

    OutcomeLine

    Line under a browsing dialog's list: what is under way (its progress), else how the last thing went

    PathRow

    Where a browsing dialog's list is: ↑ UP while inside a folder, then the kind and the folders opened ("SAMPLES / kicks / 909"); the owner's buttons at the right

    PickUp

    How the fixed pots (Z2_V5B) take over a value they do not sit on

    Plate

    Plate in a screen's header band, its first row

    SecondPress

    What waits for a confirming second press, as ravving asks before anything it cannot take back (opening over unsaved changes, DELETE)

    SectionHeader

    Heading of a collapsible machine section ("▾ SOUND MODIFIERS", "▸ SOUND EFFECTS")

    SideBarItem

    A row of a page's sidebar: a glyph and a name; the one shown on a soft tint with an accent edge

    StripButton

    A button of a machine's strip (REC, pause, STEP, LIVE, S, M, and a page's own actions such as RANDOM)

    Toast

    A short notice at the bottom centre of the screen, in ravving's toast style

    ValueBubble

    Value bubble: the value of the control being changed, large, above it

    ValueTip

    What the value bubble shows: the control being changed, its name and its value
