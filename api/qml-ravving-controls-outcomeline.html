    OutcomeLine QML Type

    The line under a browsing dialog's list: what is under way (its progress), else how the last thing went. More...

    Detailed Description

    While busy it shows status in the dim ink and, with a progress of 0..1, a thin bar along its foot; otherwise message, green when ok and red when not. Its children (a STOP) stand at its right.
