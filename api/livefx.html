    Detailed Description

    The pads, in order:

    • DROP: while held the kick goes and a high-pass builds up; let go and it all slams back;
    • NO KICK and ONLY KICK: silence every drum machine's kick row, or everything but it (Machine::setKickMode()); they are opposites, one switches the other off;
    • CRUSH: the master through a bit crusher (Calf Crusher), 6 bits; dragging a held pad picks 8, 6, 4 or 3 bits (up is harder);
    • LO PASS and HI PASS: the master sinks under a low-pass sweep (down to 300 Hz) or thins out under a high-pass (up to 950 Hz), on GlobalPlayback's own equaliser;
    • STUTTER: while held, the 16th under way plays out and then repeats (BeatRepeat); dragging up ratchets it to 1/32 and 1/64;
    • LOOP: the same with a beat, from the step under way; dragging up shortens it (2 steps, 1 step), down lengthens it (half a bar). STUTTER wins while both are held.

    NO KICK, ONLY KICK, LO PASS and HI PASS latch on a tap. DROP and CRUSH act while held, and a hold of half a second latches them too (a tap then lets go). STUTTER and LOOP act only while held (SELECT on the device latches them).

    The FX SENDS knobs: PHASER (Calf Phaser on the master), ECHO (every machine thrown into the studio's delay at least this much) and HEAVY (GlobalPlayback's compressor squashing harder).

    The master runs GlobalPlayback, the beat repeat, the crusher, the phaser, then the speakers, each passing the sound through untouched until used (masterInserts()). Without libzynthbox (a desktop) only the states change. GUI thread only.

    LiveFx::LiveFx(ZynthboxBridge *bridge, QObject *globalPlayback, const QList<Machine *> &machines, QObject *parent = nullptr)

    Constructs the LIVE FX over globalPlayback (libzynthbox's GlobalPlayback passthrough, the master; null on a desktop) and the studio's machines (kick cuts, echo throws), with the given parent. bridge runs the crusher and phaser and keeps the beat repeat on its grid (null on a desktop).

    [invokable] void LiveFx::drag(const QString &pad, int notches)

    A held CRUSH, STUTTER or LOOP pad was dragged notches up (negative: down) from where it was pressed: it picks the bit depth, ratchet or loop length from its starting one.

    Note: This function can be invoked via the meta-object system and from QML. See Q_INVOKABLE.

    [invokable] void LiveFx::key(const QString &pad, bool down)

    The device's ALT + step key of pad went down (down true) or came up: DROP, STUTTER and LOOP act only while the key is held, the other pads flip as the key goes down.

    Note: This function can be invoked via the meta-object system and from QML. See Q_INVOKABLE.

    [signal] void LiveFx::knobsChanged()
