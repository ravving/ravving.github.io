    Detailed Description

    The pads come in two banks of BankSize (banks(), bank()); pads() lists the shown bank's, an empty name for an empty slot, and allPads() every pad. MAIN is headerPads(), the sixteen the profile keeps (liveFx/header; the first eight stand in the header, the second eight show while ALT is held), by default:

    • MAIN: DROP (while held the kick is cut and a high-pass sweeps up; on release both return), NO KICK and ONLY KICK (silence every drum machine's kick row, or everything but it, Machine::setKickMode(); exclusive), CRUSH (the master through a bit crusher, Calf Crusher, 6 bits; drag() picks 8, 6, 4 or 3 bits), LO PASS and HI PASS (a low-pass sweep down to 300 Hz or a high-pass sweep up to 950 Hz, on GlobalPlayback's own equaliser), STUTTER (while held, the current 16th plays out and then repeats, BeatRepeat; drag() shortens it to 1/32 and 1/64), LOOP 4 (the same with a beat, from the current step); then LOOP 8 (an 8th), KILL LOW, KILL MID and KILL HIGH (the isolator's three bands cut, LiveFxEngine), REVERSE and STOP (the engine's heads: the beat before played backwards, the tape slowing to a stop), ECHO and FREEZE;
    • MORE, the rest: LOOP 8T, LOOP 16 and LOOP 32 (an 8th triplet, a 16th, a 32nd), REWIND, HALF, OCT UP and WOBBLE (the other heads), FADE OUT; then FLANGER, GATE, AUTO-PAN, MONO, PHONE and ALIAS.

    One LOOP plays at a time, STUTTER on top of a held one; one head reads the engine's ring at a time: a press on one lets go of the others of its group.

    NO KICK, ONLY KICK, LO PASS, HI PASS and the KILLs toggle on press(). DROP, CRUSH, ALIAS, PHONE, MONO, FLANGER, GATE and AUTO-PAN act while pressed, and a press held for 0.5 s latches them (a later short press releases them). STUTTER, the LOOPs, the heads, ECHO, FREEZE and FADE OUT act only while pressed, unless latched with toggle().

    The send levels: phaser (Calf Phaser on the master), echo (every machine sent into the studio's delay at least this much) and heavy (GlobalPlayback's compressor squashing harder).

    The master chain is GlobalPlayback, the beat repeat (with the engine after it), the crusher and the phaser, each passing the sound through untouched until used (masterInserts()). Without libzynthbox (a desktop run) only the states change. GUI thread only.

    headerPads : QStringList

    This property holds the sixteen slots ALT + the step keys reach, pad names in order: the first eight stand in the header, the second eight show while ALT is held. Kept in the profile as liveFx/header; a name that is no pad or repeats is dropped, and short lists are filled from the default order. MORE is every other pad.

    LiveFx::LiveFx(ZynthboxBridge *bridge, int *master, const QList<Machine *> &machines, QObject *parent = nullptr)

    Constructs the live effects over master (libravving's master strip, GlobalPlayback; null on a desktop run) and the studio's machines (kick cuts, echo sends), with the given parent. bridge runs the crusher and phaser and keeps the beat repeat and the engine on the transport's grid (null on a desktop run).

    int LiveFx::audioSampleRate() const

    Returns the audio clock's sample rate (BeatRepeat::sampleRate()); 0 on a desktop run.

    [signal] void LiveFx::bankChanged()

    Emitted when bank, and so pads, changed.

    Note: Notifier signal for properties bank and pads.

    [invokable] QString LiveFx::debugState() const

    Returns one line of the pads' state and the beat repeat's (BeatRepeat::debugState()): the active pads, the engine effects asked for, and what the audio thread last did. For call studio.liveFx debugState over the fifo while a pad misbehaves; also written to the log.

    Note: This function can be invoked via the meta-object system and from QML. See Q_INVOKABLE.

    [invokable] void LiveFx::drag(const QString &pad, int notches)

    Adjusts a held CRUSH or STUTTER pad by notches (positive harder or shorter, negative the other way) from its starting setting: the bit depth or the repeat length.

    Note: This function can be invoked via the meta-object system and from QML. See Q_INVOKABLE.

    [invokable] void LiveFx::key(const QString &pad, bool down)

    Handles the hardware key of pad going down (down true) or up: DROP, STUTTER, the LOOPs, the heads, ECHO, FREEZE and FADE OUT act only while the key is held; the other pads toggle as the key goes down.

    Note: This function can be invoked via the meta-object system and from QML. See Q_INVOKABLE.

    [signal] void LiveFx::knobsChanged()

    Emitted when phaser, echo or heavy changed.

    Note: Notifier signal for properties echo, heavy, and phaser.

    QList<AudioInsert *> LiveFx::masterInserts() const

    Returns the master chain's inserts, in order, for the wiring: only those that have ports.

    [invokable] void LiveFx::release(const QString &pad)

    void LiveFx::syncGrid()

    Puts the engine's effects back on the transport's grid: the bar under way and the tempo. Call it when the tempo changes or the transport starts or stops; a pad's press does it itself.
