    ravving C++ API

    ravving is a Qt 6 application on top of libzynthbox. Its C++ side is two targets: the ravving app (src/: the window, the hardware input, the bridge to libzynthbox) and the ravving_backend static library (src/backend/: the studio, its machines, the engines, the recorders and the device services). The QML in qml/ reaches the backend through context properties (studio, ravving, zynthbox) and the Ravving QML module; it is not documented here.

    The setup program (setup/) installs the AppImage and its data on a Zynthbox; Running on a Zynthbox describes it with the service, the scripts and the system files it uses.

    The classes by area

    AreaWhat it holds
    App and hardware inputThe application object, the device's keys, knobs and LEDs, the bridge to libzynthbox and libzyncoder, the style host and the startup splash.
    Studio and projectsThe studio that owns the machines, the song's sections, the undo history, the projects, templates and presets.
    MachinesThe machine base class and the drum, mono, chord and sampler machines, with the kits and the sound controls they play through.
    Engines and effectsThe sound engines (jalv, FluidSynth), the LV2 inserts and the effect chains.
    Audio, recording and exportThe JACK graph and its wiring, the outputs, the taps and meters, the recorders, the tuner, the exporter and the music player.
    Device, network and filesDevice controls and checks, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, the computer link, the file server, USB sticks, the file browser and the paths.
    Setup programThe installer of the AppImage and its data.

    Conventions

    • Threads: unless a member says otherwise it runs on the GUI thread. Members called from JACK's process callback say so ("audio thread").
    • Ownership: QObjects are parented to their owner; a member that hands out a pointer says who owns it.
    • Units: each member says its own: a level is 0..1 of its range or dB, a time milliseconds or seconds, a position in a pattern steps or ticks.
