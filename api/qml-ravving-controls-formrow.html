    Detailed Description

    The rows of a settings pane (design-system B24): the name in the label step, the line under it in the body step, dim; the control (a Chip, a row of Chips, an ActionButton, a value) given as the row's children, at the right edge. A row whose control is wide (a long row of choices) sets stacked and puts it under the words instead. ringed draws the device's ring while the keys are on the row; divider a hairline under it.
