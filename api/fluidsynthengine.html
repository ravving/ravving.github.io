    Detailed Description

    It runs with FluidSynth's usual Zynthian options plus the soundfont itself on the command line (so it is font 1 and no default GM soundfont loads), select for the preset, and the MIDI router sending every incoming channel to channel 0. It renders at the graph's rate and period (AudioGraph). The process is a ConsoleProcess (libzynthbox's ProcessWrapper): restarted if it crashes, and it dies with ravving.

    kits.json engine object: {"type": "fluidsynth", "soundfont": path, "bank": 0, "program": 0}.

    Its controls (a soundfont source's sound controls, kits.json sourceSound) are named cc:N, a MIDI controller (0..127; CC 1 is the vibrato of the SoundFont's default modulator, at most 50 cents), or gen:N, an offset to SoundFont 2 generator N of every voice on the channel, in the generator's own units (cents for the filter cutoff, centibels for its resonance, timecents for the envelope's times), sent as the SF2 NRPN FluidSynth understands (NRPN 120 / N, data entry around 8192, scaled by the generator's NRPN step). Both hold for the notes played after them and are sent again at every start. A preset whose vibrato LFO is stopped (a rate under about 1 Hz) gets it started at 5.5 Hz, read from the soundfont itself (SoundFontFile), so vibrato reaches it.
