    LiveRecMic Class

    The live recording's own input path, published to QML as video.input: the mic (or guitar, or line) as the recording hears it, with its own mode, gain, role and effects, apart from the RECORDER's (InputRecorder). More...

    Detailed Description

    • The line: while wanted (setWanted(), VideoRecorder: LIVE REC on with MIC) it holds the device input (InputRecorder::hold()) so nothing else records, monitors or processes the jacks meanwhile, and gives it back when not (release()). A hold is refused while a RECORDER take is in progress: status is busy then, and the line is taken as soon as the take ends.
    • The path: while holding, the card gets this path's gain and bias (InputCard::apply()), and the jack in use (InputRecorder::activeJack(), followed) goes through its own InputEffects (video.input.effects, the presets with uses liverec among them) into the listener (AudioTap's mic ports: setListener()). It has no monitor and no take of its own: the speakers never get it (a mic by the speakers howls), the tap does.
    • The role: voiceover (the music ducks under the voice, the VOICE-OVER chain) or singing (the music stays, the SINGING chain). Picking one loads its chain; the effects can be changed afterwards and the role keeps saying how the mix behaves (ducks()).

    Mode, gains, role and the effects' preset and knobs are the live recording's own settings, kept in QSettings (ravving/video and ravving/liverec-fx), apart from the RECORDER's. GUI thread only.
