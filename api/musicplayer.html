    Detailed Description

    A QMediaPlayer with a QAudioOutput decodes WAV, FLAC, Ogg and MP3 (FFmpeg). Its stream goes to PipeWire beside the studio's JACK graph, not through libzynthbox, into the sink set with setOutputSink(). Starting the studio's transport pauses the player.

    The queue: playNow() inserts a track after the current one and plays it, enqueue() appends, playAt() jumps to an entry, next() and previous() step, and a track that ends starts the next one (wrapping round when repeat is on). pause() keeps the position; seek() moves within the track.

    The QMediaPlayer and its QAudioOutput exist only while a track is loaded: they are created when playback starts and deleted by stop() and at the end of the queue, so no decoder or PipeWire stream stays open while nothing plays. GUI thread only.
