    A Machine playing a monophonic line; kits.json configures the bass and the lead as two instances

    PadKitControls

    PadSamplerMachine's kit controls: the knobs that belong to the kit rather than to one pad

    PadSamplerMachine

    A Machine playing 16 sample pads on libzynthbox's SamplerSynth, in four banks (A to D) of 16 pads and steps each

    SamplerBanks

    Samplers' banks as a section sees them

    SamplerMachine

    A Machine playing samples on libzynthbox's SamplerSynth, with four banks (A to D) of eight sample slots and steps each

    SoundControls

    A machine's sound controls: cards of knobs that each drive its engine's own controls (LV2 ports of a jalv engine, FluidSynth's cc: and gen: controls, by symbol)

    SoundControls::Card

    One card: its title and its knobs, in order

    SoundControls::Choice

    One position of a switch knob: its label and the engine controls it sets (symbol to value)

    SoundControls::Knob

    One knob: its name, the engine control it drives (symbol), its range there (minimum, maximum), its defaultValue (0..1), its choices when it is a switch (empty for a continuous knob) and whether it maps exponentially (logarithmic)
