    ControllerId Class

    What names a controller, whichever way it is plugged in: a known controller's label (ControllerProfiles), the same for its cable and its radio, else the normalised name of its one connection (ConnectionRegistry::keyOf()). More...

    Detailed Description

    Everything kept or keyed per controller (its settings, its driver, its mixer, its octave's link) is kept by it; QML sees it as a string (toString()).
