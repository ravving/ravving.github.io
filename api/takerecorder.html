    Detailed Description

    One take at a time. Starting one:

    • with the transport stopped, the pattern is cleared and the transport starts; its first bar is the count-in (the metronome ticks it), and the take records from that bar's last 16th, so a hit played just before the downbeat lands on it;
    • with the transport playing, it waits for the machine's loop to come round, then clears the pattern and records from its first step.

    While recording, every live hit (hit()) is written at the nearest step, loop after loop: the first pass is the fresh take, later passes overdub. toggle() again or stop() ends it; the take is one undo step (Machine::beginEditGroup()).

    The metronome ticks through a take: switched on for it if it was off, and back off when the take ends, unless it was switched manually meanwhile. The take records notes, not audio. GUI thread only.
