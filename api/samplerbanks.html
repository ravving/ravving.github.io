    Detailed Description

    SamplerMachine and PadSamplerMachine keep banks in their state: a list of maps with steps, accents, bars and more, with each bank's samples under one key (the pads' pads, the slots' slots). A section keeps every bank's hits and leaves the samples to the machine, since loading a sample cannot happen on the bar line; the bank that plays is the machine's current one, not the section's, since a bank change reloads samples. Pure functions on plain data.
