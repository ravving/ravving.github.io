Shortcuts

One card per region of the panel. Hover or tap a row to see it on the screen; the picture's frame points at what to look at.

  1. 1 – 5 open a machine: drums, chords, bass, lead or the samplers.
  2. The step keys write the beat on the picked row; ▲ ▼ pick the row.
  3. PLAY to hear it, STOP to stop.
  4. The big knob turns the machine on screen up or down.
  5. SAVE, in the header, keeps your project.

The same five, step by step: your first beat. Every other key is in the cards below.

Tap any key on the panel to see what it does, with ALT and without.

1

Knobs

3 knobs · big knob · X Y
  • BIG Volume of the machine on screenInside a page the big knob is always the fader of the instrument shown; the fader wears the big-knob badge.
  • ALT+BIG Master volume, from anywhereBadged on the header's MASTER dial; it never reaches the page, whatever has the focus.
  • 123 The focused group's knobsA group of dials hands its targets to knobs 1–3; each target shows a tiny knob badge with its number. The big knob steps the group's list (INSTRUMENTS: knob 1) and SELECT activates.
  • ALT+123 FX SENDS: PHASER, ECHO, HEAVYThe header's sends, borrowed while ALT is held: badges, frame and pick-up move there.
  • SELECTthen123 A note's LENGTH, VELOCITY, NUDGEOn the bass and lead, with SELECT on and notes picked. Knob 1 sets the length, from a step to 4 bars. Knob 2 sets the velocity, and knob 3 nudges the notes up to half a step early or late. The grid's top right shows the values.
  • stripthen12 PAN and VOL of the machineTap a machine's strip: knob 1 pans, knob 2 sets the level. BACK, or a tap in the grid, gives the knobs back to the rows.
  • ✱then12BIG In the MIXER: level, pan, fine levelOf the strip picked (◀ ▶ or a step key): knob 1 its level, knob 2 its pan, the big knob a fine level.
  • MENUthen12 In the control center: BRIGHTNESS, OUTPUTKnob 1 the screen's brightness, knob 2 the output level. A fixed pot picks the value up first: it changes nothing until it reaches or crosses it, and the brightness never goes under 30 %, so the screen never looks switched off.
  • RECORDERthenBIG In the RECORDER: the input GAINThe number keys move knobs 1–3 between INPUT (1), TUNER (2) and EFFECTS (3).
  • XY The previous / next sectionFrom any page or dialog, wrapping round: picked as a section key would (from the next bar while playing, at once when stopped; the plate blinks while it waits). Pressed again before the switch lands, the key moves the pick on instead of queueing another.

Page keys

MENU · 1–5 · ✱ · MODE
  • MENU The control centerThe MIXER, Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, MIDI, the RECORDER, the music player, SETTINGS and more. ◀ ▶ walk through them and SELECT opens one. REBOOT and POWER OFF ask for a second press.
  • 1 DRUMSPressed again it cycles the three drum machines: DRUMS 1 → 2 → 3 (the nameplate says which).
  • 2 CHORDSThe chords page: CHORDS, NOTES and SLOTS views. Pressed again it turns them: CHORDS → NOTES → SLOTS.
  • 3 BASSThe BASS page: Nekobi, a 303, a mono line with ACCENT and SLIDE.
  • 4 LEADCalf Monosynth, legato and portamento.
  • 5 SAMPLERPressed again it switches SLOT SAMPLER ⇄ PAD SAMPLER; the choice survives a restart.
  • ✱ The MIXER, open and closeEvery strip at once; a number key leaves it for that page, BACK or ✱ go back.
  • MODE The entry mode of the instrument on screenOFF → LIVE → STEP + LIVE → OFF, from anywhere; the MODE LED shows it. LIVE turns the step keys into pads for that one machine.
  • ALT+MODE Flip the header deckThe section keys take the place of the LIVE FX pads (the same again brings the pads back); MODE lights orange while ALT is held. A long press on the project plate does the same.
  • RECORDERthen123 In the RECORDER: INPUT, TUNER, EFFECTSMove the knobs between the three panels.

Step keys

1–16
  • 1…16 Write a stepLIVE off: the key toggles the selected row's step on release, one key a step of the bar on screen. The LEDs follow the playhead.
  • MODEthen1…16 Play, with LIVE onOn the drums, keys 1–8 play the rows and holding key 9 adds accents. On the bass, lead and NOTES, the keys play the scale, and ▲ ▼ change the octave. On CHORDS, they play the seven chords. With REC on, what you play is written down.
  • ALT+1…16 The LIVE FX padsKeys 1–8 are the eight pads in the header, keys 9–16 the eight that take the instruments' place while ALT is held. The keys light orange while ALT is held, the pads show their key numbers, and a pad shows pressed while its key is down.
  • ALT+▼ The next bank of LIVE FX padsMAIN, then MORE. The banks' names, shown while ALT is held, do the same by touch.
  • ALT+MODEthenALT+1…16 Sections 1–16, from any pageWith the section keys in the header, ALT + 1–16 pick them, lit in their colours while ALT is held.
  • ✱then1…9 In the MIXER: pick a stripThe step LEDs show the strips, muted ones dim, the picked one white.
  • 1…16 PAD SAMPLER: the sixteen padsWith LIVE on the keys play the pads; a key held past half a second latches its pad looping, a key on a latched pad ends it. STOP ends every latched loop.
  • 1+5 Keys held together go into one stepSTEP entry: a chord in one column.

Transport

● ▶ metronome ■
  • ▶ PlayThe loop, or the song through in SONG mode. Playing locks the note grids (LOCK) so a stray touch cannot change what plays; the PLAY LED is bright while it plays.
  • ■ StopUnlocks the grids again. While VIDEO is on it ends the recording (or cancels the countdown) and leaves the music playing; it also ends the PAD SAMPLER's latched loops and a recording picked in the RECORDER.
  • ■while stopped Panic: all notes offEvery instrument's held notes end (a note left sounding).
  • ● Record a take of the machine on screenStopped, ● counts you in for a bar. Playing, it punches in when the loop comes round, and keeps layering loop after loop. The RECORD light takes the machine's colour.
  • ALT+● Record the whole mixThe global recording (the header's ●, red RECORD LED), from anywhere; RECORD with the header focused does the same.
  • VIDEOthen●/■ Start and end a live recordingWith LIVE REC on in the control center, RECORD starts recording the screen or a USB camera, with the sound, from any page. STOP ends it and the music; STOP again within three seconds leaves LIVE REC. The plate shows ● REC and the time.
  • ♩ Metronomelibzynthbox's tick and tock on the master; a take holds it on.
  • RECORDERthen●▶■ In the RECORDER: a take, hear, stopRECORD a free take of the input; SELECT or PLAY hears the recording picked; STOP stops it. ◀ ▶ the input, ▲ ▼ the recordings.

Navigation

BACK · ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶ · SELECT
  • ▲▼ Walk the pageOne vertical order per machine: header → strip → MODS cards (while open) → the editor → SOUND EFFECTS cards (while open). The editor moves its rows first (a drum row, a note row).
  • ▲at the top Enter the header▲ past the strip lands on the header's first group. There ◀ ▶ move between the groups (key, transport, tempo, LIVE FX, INSTRUMENTS, MASTER) and ▼ or BACK return to the page.
  • ALT+▲ The header at once, from anywhereFrom a page's rows, a section's cards or the strip.
  • ◀▶ The bar pages, the STEP cursorOn a grid: the previous / next bar page, as the toolbar's ‹ › (which show pressed). In STEP entry: the cursor a column, on into the next page, stopping at the pattern's ends. On the samplers: the bank or tab. In the SOUND EFFECTS bar: the chips.
  • BACKNOon a grid UndoThe machine's last change, as its toolbar's ↶ (which shows pressed). In STEP entry the cursor goes to the column undone. With notes picked, BACK drops the picks first. ALT + BACK leaves the grid for the page.
  • SELECTYES Write at the cursor · switch · runSTEP entry, stopped: writes the editor's selected row (a note, or the chord on CHORDS) at the cursor. In a SOUND EFFECTS card or its bar: switches the effect on or off. In the control center: runs the action ringed.
  • SELECTthen▲▼ Move the picked notesWith SELECT on, ▲ ▼ move the picked notes up or down the scale, or by a semitone with ♭♯ on. ◀ ▶ move them a step. A held chord moves as one.
  • MODElive▲▼ Octave, or the rows by earLIVE on a melodic page: ▲ ▼ shift the octave of the step keys; OCT + / − show pressed and the OCT readout flashes. LIVE and STEP on: they walk the rows instead, each sounding as it is reached.
  • ✱then◀▶SELECTYES In the MIXER: pick, mute, solo◀ ▶ pick a strip, SELECT mutes it, ALT + SELECT solos it; BACK or ✱ go back.
  • BACKNO Back, close, give backCloses what's open and takes you back a step: out of the header, the MIXER, the SONG page or the RECORDER, or out of a dialog.
  • SONGthen◀▶▲▼ On the SONG page◀ ▶ walk along, and ▲ ▼ jump between the sections and the song. SELECT or a step key picks one. ALT + ◀ ▶ moves a block, knob 1 sets its length and knob 2 its repeats.
  • ALT+SELECTYES In a sound picker: ★ favouriteMarks the sound ringed as a favourite (the ★ group).

Features

The highlights first, then everything else in groups below. Each one links to its page in the docs.

Pages 1–5 / eight machines

Five keys. Eight machines.

Each number key opens a page, and every page is an instrument.

  • Drums on 1 three drum machines that start on a 909, an 808 and a Linn kit, or any kit you like.
  • Chords, bass, lead on 2, 3 and 4: TAL NoiseMaker, a 303-style bass and Calf Monosynth.
  • Samplers on 5 two of them, below.
  • The same on every page a pattern editor, MODS and a SOUND EFFECTS chain.

Page 5 / Slot Sampler · Pad Sampler

Two samplers on one key.

Key 5 opens SLOT SAMPLER or the PAD SAMPLER; press it again to switch.

  • SLOT SAMPLER four banks of eight slots: trim, pitch, reverse, record a take straight in, CHOP.
  • PAD SAMPLER sixteen MPC-style pads on the step keys, with 16 LEVELS and latched pads.
  • PAD FX ECHO, REVERSE, STUTTER, SCRATCH and TAPE STOP, held while a pad plays.

Presets / the genre packs

Presets that bring their sound.

Twenty-five packs of patterns on a USB stick, written to follow your key.

  • A template per pack a new project at its tempo, swing and key.

Sections / loop or song

Sections, and a song.

Up to sixteen sections, each holding every machine's pattern.

  • Switch on time on the next bar (BAR), or when the section ends (SECTION).
  • LOOP or SONG loop the one on screen, or play the song through, repeats and all.
  • One take EXPORT records the whole song.

Recorder / the tuner

Record what you play. Tune the guitar.

Plug in a mic, a guitar or a line, and record while the beat keeps going.

  • Guitar effects amps and pedals, from OVERDRIVE and FUZZ to WAH.
  • Your takes trim, normalise, chop, or send them to SLOT SAMPLER or the pads.
  • The TUNER the note and how far off it is, with fifteen tunings for guitar, bass and ukulele.

Live recording / the session, with your voice

Record the session as it happens.

Tap LIVE REC in the menu, and the whole session goes to a file.

  • Video the screen or a USB camera, with the music in sync.
  • Sound only a 24-bit WAV, ready to tag with a title, artist and cover.
  • Voice over plug in a mic and the music ducks while you talk.
  • When it ends delete it, rename it, or send it on.

Sing over it: the music makes room for your voice.

Share / USB · computer · phone · video

Out of the device, three ways.

One SEND… button, wherever there's a file.

  • A USB stick into its ravving folder.
  • Your computers over SSH, set up once with a password.
  • Your phone scan a QR code: no app and no pairing, so an iPhone works too.
  • FILES browse everything on the device, from the phone too.

Export / the music player

Export the track. Play it back.

PROJECT ▾ → EXPORT writes your track just as the speakers play it.

  • Any format WAV, FLAC, OGG or MP3, with tags and a cover.
  • A .ravv bundle every sample included, so another ravving plays it the same.
  • The MUSIC PLAYER your tracks full screen, through the jacks, HDMI or Bluetooth.

Mixtape / your tracks, one album

Make a mixtape. Hand it over.

Give a few tracks the same ALBUM / MIXTAPE when you export them, and the MUSIC PLAYER puts them together under ALBUMS.

  • One name type it once: EXPORT offers it again next time.
  • A cover each a night-city artwork with your title down a black sidebar, who made it and what plays, and maybe a sticker. Tap it in EXPORT for another.
  • Take it with you send the tracks to a USB stick, a computer or your phone.

Engines / open source, top to bottom

Open source. Nothing extra.

ravving is open source: a Qt 6 app on libzynthbox, GPL-3.0-only (its LIVE FX engine adapts code from SLOOP, by Hügelton Instruments).

  • Sounds from the system jalv for the LV2 synths and effects, FluidSynth for the kits and soundfonts.
  • Nothing extra it uses what the Zynthbox image already has, so the AppImage bundles none of it.

Every feature, grouped

Open a group; each line links to the page that explains it.

  • DRUMS 1 · 2 · 3Eight rows, from KICK to PERC, and an accent row. Each row has its own mute, solo and level. Pick an electronic kit, or an acoustic one: Jazz, Brush, Room, Orchestra and more. engines
  • CHORDSSeven chord keys that follow your key and scale. SLOTS writes a progression with whole chords, 1 to 8 bars each. The sound is TAL NoiseMaker or a soundfont. manual
  • BASSA mono line on a piano roll with ACCENT and SLIDE lanes, OCT − / +, ♭♯, RANDOM; the 303's WAVE, CUTOFF, RESO, ENV MOD, ACCENT, TUNE as its MODS. engines
  • LEADLegato and portamento on Calf Monosynth, a ribbon that plays and adds vibrato as it is dragged up, soundfont instruments from Rhodes and vibes to sax, flute, fiddle and bagpipe. engines
  • SLOT SAMPLERFour banks × eight slots, each bank with its pattern; trim, PITCH ±12, REVERSE, record into a slot, the input's effects, CHOP and AUTO CHOP. manual
  • PAD SAMPLERSixteen pads on the step keys. Record straight onto a pad, play it once, loop it or reverse it, and hold a pad to latch it. The wave editor cuts a sample into pads. manual
  • Sound sourcesBass, lead and chords can sound like a soundfont instrument instead of their synth, grouped in the picker with ★ favourites and a SORT; an imported soundfont's presets join at once. manual
  • MODSThe knobs that shape the sound: FILTER, ENVELOPE, MOTION… for a soundfont, the synths' own knobs for a synth. The device knobs drive them, and a project or a preset keeps them. engines
  • SOUND EFFECTSEvery machine's chain: DRIVE, COMPRESSOR, REVERB, EQ, PAN, SIDECHAIN, DELAY, each a card with its knobs, switched from the bar or the SELECT key. architecture
  • LIVE FXThirty pads on the whole mix, in two banks on ALT + the step keys: cuts, kills and sweeps, five loop lengths, reverse, rewind, tape stop, echo, freeze and the tone tricks. PHASER, ECHO and HEAVY as sends on ALT + the knobs. manual
  • Key, scale, tempo, swingEvery melodic machine follows the root and scale (a key change snaps the lines); SWING 8 or SWING 16; the tempo on the header, templates and demos setting it. manual
  • LIVE and takesMODE turns the step keys into pads for the machine on screen. ● records what you play, layering loop after loop. A MIDI keyboard plays along too. manual
  • The MIXEREvery strip at once (✱): level, pan, mute, solo, the step keys picking a strip, the big knob a fine level. manual

  • The pattern editorsA step grid for the drums and samplers, a piano roll for the bass and lead, and a chord roll. Patterns run up to eight bars. LOCK stops a stray tap from changing what plays. manual
  • SELECT and the note knobsPick notes and move them along the scale or by a step. Knobs 1–3 set their LENGTH, VELOCITY and NUDGE. manual
  • PresetsSave a pattern with its sound and tweaks. Browse by genre, star your favourites, and play presets straight from a USB stick or online, without saving them. manual
  • WITH ITS SOUND · MODS · BARSWhat a preset brings with it: its sound source or kit, its MODS (only the parts it names), its bar count (or laid over the pattern's bars). manual
  • The genre packsTwenty-five packs, most with five presets per instrument. Each pack sticks to one scale, so its parts play well together. Some are in 3/4 and 6/8. the packs
  • TemplatesPROJECT ▾ → TEMPLATES: a new project at a tempo, swing, key and LIVE FX, from the device, a USB stick or online; SAVE TEMPLATE keeps the session's own; every pack ships one. manual
  • GET MOREGenre packs, presets, skins, soundfonts and demo projects to download from the catalog, straight into the device; UPDATE when a newer one is listed, REMOVE to take it out. Every picker has a GET MORE… button, and the presets dialog's ONLINE plays any of the catalog's presets without downloading the rest. manual
  • DemosAutumn Leaves (a jazz trio and sax), 5AM (dark electronic pop over the changes of 4ÆM) and B3rlin (dark techno), on the image's own soundfonts, never changed by a SAVE. manual
  • ProjectsSAVE (the name asked once, AGAIN? to write over), SAVE AS NEW, RENAME, ★ favourites, DEMOS and MY PROJECTS groups; a new project starts clean. manual
  • Project iconsGive a project a pixel icon when you save or rename it: it sits beside the name in PROJECT ▾, travels in a bundle and wears the exported track's cover. manual
  • Undo and redoOne history for the project (the header's ↶ ↷): patterns, slots and the arrangement, a change over several machines as one step; a machine's own ↶ ↷ undo its changes alone. undo
  • AutomationAUTO records knob moves into lanes per machine; a long press on AUTO clears them. architecture
  • By scriptA whole project built over the remote-control fifo: samples, patterns, sounds and all. scripting

  • SectionsUp to sixteen, each with every machine's pattern. A shorter pattern loops (LOOP) or plays once (ONCE). Sounds, effects, tempo and key stay the same, so switching never drops a note. manual
  • SWITCH · BAR | SECTIONA pick lands on the next bar, or waits for the section playing to end its pass; in LOOP up to three picks queue, each playing one pass. manual
  • On the bar line, exactlyEach machine writes the new pattern into its track's other clip and libzynthbox swaps the two on the bar's first tick: no note drops or doubles (measured). design
  • From anywhereX and Y step through the sections from any page; ALT + MODE shows all sixteen in the header, where ALT + step keys 1–16 pick them. manual
  • The SONG pageEvery section as a key, with a dot for each machine that plays. Rename, colour and reorder them, then line them up into a song with repeats. PLAY FROM HERE starts anywhere. manual
  • LOOP | SONGWhat PLAY plays: the section on screen looped, or the song through, the pages following it, stopping on the bar line after its end. manual
  • Seeing the changeThe plate sweeps in the section's colour and the page glows round its frame as a change lands; the keys fill with the section's progress. manual

  • The RECORDERA mic, a guitar or a line on jacks 4 and 5, with a level meter. Voice effects like ROBOT and TELEPHONE, and amps and pedals for the guitar. manual
  • The tunerOn GUITAR: McLeod's pitch method as guitarix's tuner has it, AUTO or MANUAL strings, fifteen tunings, A4 430–450 Hz; it runs only while on screen. manual
