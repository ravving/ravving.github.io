MIKO M01 / ravving · the groovebox mod

Every key.
Every knob.
In ravving.

MIKO is the hardware: an open music instrument by Zynthbox with sixteen step keys, five page keys, three encoders and a jog wheel. ravving is the alternative app, a mod, that runs on it: a groovebox of five pages (drums, chords, bass, lead, sampler) with sections, a mixer and a recorder. This page is the map of what the panel does while ravving is the app on screen, and of what the app can do.

Knobs

  • 123 The focused group's knobsA group of dials hands its targets to knobs 1–3; each target shows a tiny knob badge with its number. The big knob steps the group's list (INSTRUMENTS: knob 1) and SELECT activates.

  • ▲at the top Enter the header▲ past the strip lands on the header's first group. There ◀ ▶ move between the groups (key, transport, tempo, LIVE FX, INSTRUMENTS, MASTER) and ▼ or BACK return to the page.
  • ALT+▲ The header at once, from anywhereFrom a page's rows, a section's cards or the strip.
  • ◀▶ What the page switchesThe CHORDS views with the editor focused, the drum machine, the sampler's bank; the keys light while they switch something. In STEP entry they move the entry cursor a step. In the SOUND EFFECTS bar they ring the chips.
  • ALT+◀/▶ Undo / redoThe project's timeline, as the header's ↶ ↷: patterns, slots and the arrangement; a change that touched several machines is one step. Sounds, the mix and the settings are not undone.
  • XY The previous / next sectionFrom any page or dialog, wrapping round: picked as a section key would (from the next bar while playing, at once when stopped; the plate blinks while it waits). Pressed again before the switch lands, the key moves the pick on instead of queueing another.

  • SELECTthen▲▼ Move the picked notesWith the toolbar's SELECT on, ▲ ▼ move every picked note one note of the key up or down (a semitone with ♭♯ on), keeping length, accent and slide; ◀ ▶ move them a step. A held chord moves as one.

  • ✱then◀▶SELECTYES In the MIXER: pick, mute, solo◀ ▶ pick a strip, SELECT mutes it, ALT + SELECT solos it; BACK or ✱ go back.
  • BACKNO Back, close, give backCloses an open section and returns the focus to the editor; gives the strip's knobs back to the rows; leaves the header, the MIXER, the SONG page, the RECORDER; dismisses a dialog.
  • SONGthen◀▶▲▼ On the SONG page◀ ▶ walk, ▲ ▼ between the section keys and the song's blocks, SELECT or a step key picks, ALT + ◀ ▶ moves a block, knob 1 its length, knob 2 its repeats, BACK leaves.

Features

The highlights, each with its page on the device; every feature is in the groups under them, and the docs explain how everything works.

Pages 1–5 / eight machines

Five keys. Eight machines.

Key 1 cycles three drum machines, each a FluidSynth kit: a Roland TR909, a TR808 and a LINN9000 to start, any kit from the device's folder from the nameplate. 2 is CHORDS on TAL NoiseMaker, 3 BASS on a 303 (DISTRHO Nekobi), 4 LEAD on Calf Monosynth, 5 the samplers. Every machine has its strip, its SOUND MODIFIERS, a pattern editor and a SOUND EFFECTS chain; the header holds the key and scale, the transport, tempo and swing, the LIVE FX and the master.

Page 5 / Sample King · Pad Sampler

Two samplers on one key.

SAMPLE KING: four banks of eight slots with their own pattern, each slot trimmed, pitched or reversed, a take recorded straight into it, CHOP and AUTO CHOP over the bank. Press 5 again for the PAD SAMPLER: sixteen MPC-style pads on the step keys, record to a pad with SMART stop, loops and latched pads, 16 LEVELS, PAD FX held while a pad plays (ECHO, REVERSE, SKIP, STUTTER, SCRATCH, TAPE STOP) and a wave editor the two share.

Presets / the genre packs

Presets that bring their sound.

Seventeen packs of pattern presets for a USB stick, five patterns for each of the drums, the bass, the lead and the chords, written in scale degrees so they follow the project's key. A preset says ♪ and the sound it was written for, and + MODS when it keeps its tweaks; WITH ITS SOUND, WITH ITS MODS and WITH ITS BARS at the top decide what loads. Each pack ships a template: a new project at its tempo, swing, key and LIVE FX.

Sections / loop or song

Sections, and a song.

A section holds every machine's pattern at once: up to sixteen, picked from any page with ALT and a step key, switched sample-accurately on the next bar (BAR) or when the section playing has finished its pass (SECTION), with up to three queued. LOOP plays the section on screen; SONG plays the blocks through, each with its repeats, and EXPORT records the whole song in one take.

Recorder / the tuner

Record anything. Tune the guitar.

The RECORDER takes the input (MIC · GUITAR · LINE) as played while the beat goes on, through effects made for it (on GUITAR the amps and pedals: AMP, OVERDRIVE, FUZZ, TUBE, WAH, HOT AMP), and keeps the takes beside the samplers or the tracks: PLAY, TRIM, NORMALISE, CHOP, TO SAMPLE KING, TO PADS. The TUNER reads the dry input: the note and its cents on a ±50 scale, AUTO walking the strings from the lowest, fifteen tunings for guitar, bass and ukulele, A4 from 430 to 450 Hz.

Share / USB · computer · phone · video

Out of the device, three ways.

One SEND… button wherever a file is: a USB stick's ravving folder, the computer (a laptop over SSH, set up once with its password) or the phone, by a QR code of a link served by ravving itself: no app, no pairing, so an iPhone works too. The VIDEO card records the screen or a USB camera with the master's sound, RECORD and STOP starting and ending it from any page. FILES browses the library by kind, and a stick's samples, soundfonts, presets and projects come in from the USB page. This page too: SETTINGS → HELP shows its address as a QR code.

Export / the music player

Export the track. Play it back.

PROJECT ▾ → EXPORT writes the production as the speakers hear it, in WAV 24‑bit, FLAC, OGG or MP3, with its tags and a cover drawn for it, or a .ravv bundle with every sample for another ravving to play the same. The MUSIC PLAYER plays the exported tracks and the recordings full screen, by title, album or artist, with a queue, through the jacks, HDMI or a Bluetooth speaker.

Engines / open source, top to bottom

Nothing bundled. Everything open.

ravving is a C++ Qt 6 app on libzynthbox, GPL‑3.0‑or‑later, and plays what the Zynthbox image ships: jalv hosting the LV2 synths and effects, FluidSynth 2 for the drum kits and the soundfont instruments, PipeWire's JACK at 48 kHz and 256 frames. It starts and wires them at run time; the AppImage bundles none of it.
