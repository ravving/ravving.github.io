What's a MIKO?

MIKO M01 is an open music instrument, powered by Zynthbox. ravving is an app that runs on it: a groovebox, next to MIKO's own app.

All you need is a MIKO running Zynthbox, online for the install. How to get ravving

01 the machines

Eight machines, five keys

Three drum machines, chords, bass, lead and two samplers. Each has its own page and its own colour, one key away.

Meet the machines

02 sections

From loop to song

Keep the good parts of your jam as sections, switch between them on the beat, and play them back as a song.

How songs work

03 out of the box

Record and share

Export the track, film the session, and send both to a stick, a computer or your phone. No account needed.

Ways to share

5 steps

From a beat to a finished track

The whole trip, one guide page per step. Most people get through it in an evening.

  1. Tap a beatDrums on the step keys, a bass line on top, then save.
  2. Bring in the bandLoad a preset or a genre pack and every machine plays along.
  3. Make it a songKeep the good parts as sections and put them in order.
  4. Play it liveSwitch sections on the beat and push the LIVE FX pads.
  5. Export and shareA tagged track with its cover, on a stick, a computer or your phone.
