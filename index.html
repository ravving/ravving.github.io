A groovebox for your MIKO.

Drums, chords, bass, lead and two samplers, on five keys. Tap some steps, press play, and you're making music.

Get it from the Zynthbox Store

Or download the latest AppImage and open it on your MIKO.

A whole band

Three drum machines, chords, bass, lead and two samplers, each on its own page.

From loop to song

Split your jam into sections, switch them on the bar line, and line them up into a song.

Record and share

Export a track, record a video of your session, and send it to a stick, a computer or your phone.
